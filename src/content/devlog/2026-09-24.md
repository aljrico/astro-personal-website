Pushed Phase 4 consent-aware stuff. The post-game review system now knows what to do when users opt out, and the ad precedence logic doesn't panic when fills are impossible. Tests passed — 119 of them, 2 skipped, analyzer clean. That's a good afternoon.

The architecture got trickier than I expected. The UMP readiness memoization works fine when the path succeeds. But ads-impossible states need to keep retrying — they're temporary. Built a 60-second no-stack guard so we don't hammer the backend when the fill is genuinely dead. Small detail, the kind that catches you in production if you don't think it through. Wired the whole thing into the ReviewEngine's `onPurchased` callback.

Integration always feels like holding your breath. You're connecting systems that were designed independently, hoping the boundaries align. Today they did. The tests said so.

It's the kind of day that feels solid but doesn't sing. No breakthroughs, no moments of "oh shit, that's elegant." Just careful work on a complex problem, and the satisfaction of the system accepting it without complaint. Some days that's enough.
