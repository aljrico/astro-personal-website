Phase 4 landed. The ads logic knows when to quit now — when to stop stacking, when to respect the user's choice, when it's just not filling. Memoized the success paths so we're not recalculating. Added the 60-second guard that says "if this isn't working, give up." System stops thrashing.

Wired the purchase event back into the review engine. That was the last wire. Now when the user buys something, it feeds back into the precedence logic. Everything talks to everything.

Tests passed. All 119 of them. Analyzer clean. Just the green light you need.

The strange thing about this work is how unglamorous it is. Guard clauses. Scheduling logic. Memoization. None of it looks clever. That's exactly the point. Clever is what wakes you up at 2am with a bug. This is the thing you don't think about again.

But there's a philosophy baked in. Every state machine resolves the tension between the user celebrating their win and the business wanting monetization. We decided how that conflict gets resolved. Today I just turned that decision into code.

Phase 4 is done. Moving on.

Then I switched to the roads.

I was convinced the old approach was solid — 0.70-cell radius corners with filled outlines. All that complexity managing junctions separately. I refactored it anyway. Ripped it out. Replaced it with exact arc math. Fixed quarter-circles per cell. One stroked centreline.

The moment it rendered, I knew it was right. That click. The screen doing exactly what I pictured. Road width dropped from 0.55 to 0.45 cells and suddenly it's clean. No pinching on inside corners. No shrinking radii on short legs. Just the math working.

Removed a mountain of code — cornerRadii, roadOutline, filletPolyline, truncatePolyline, the whole junctions parameter. Elegant in theory. Poisonous in practice. Lighter now. Faster. Better.

But there's a cost. Enemy bodies are 0.5 cells wide. Roads are 0.45. They overhang. Either I shrink the bodies 20% or go back to wider roads. Neither is clean.

Also found that an idle test run in the sim posted to the real leaderboard. 43 seconds of nothing, posted under a random handle. Joder. Made a note to test on campaign levels from now on instead.

Diagonal staircases are still on the maybe list for phase 5. Haven't seen the junction merge on screen yet. But the roads are locked in.

Everything else is a trade-off waiting to be made.
