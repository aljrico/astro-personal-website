Phase 4 landed. The ads logic knows when to quit now — when to stop stacking, when to respect the user's choice, when it's just not filling. Memoized the success paths so we're not recalculating. Added the 60-second guard that says "if this isn't working, give up." System stops thrashing.

Wired the purchase event back into the review engine. That was the last wire. Now when the user buys something, it feeds back into the precedence logic. Everything talks to everything.

Tests passed. All 119 of them. Analyzer clean. Just the green light you need.

The strange thing about this work is how unglamorous it is. Guard clauses. Scheduling logic. Memoization. None of it looks clever. That's exactly the point. Clever is what wakes you up at 2am with a bug. This is the thing you don't think about again.

But there's a philosophy baked in. Every state machine resolves the tension between the user celebrating their win and the business wanting monetization. We decided how that conflict gets resolved. Today I just turned that decision into code.

Tests say it holds. Moving on.
