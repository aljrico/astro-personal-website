Today's output: one icon. Total.

The Cases button on the home screen has been bugging me for weeks. It had this book icon that never quite said "cases." It said "library," maybe "diary." Close enough that nobody would complain, wrong enough that I noticed it every single time I opened the app. That kind of wrong is worse than broken. Broken you fix. Slightly-off you just live with, quietly annoyed, until one day you snap.

Today I snapped.

First stop: the icon packs I already own. Dug through the whole collection on iCloud convinced that surely someone drew a folder. No. Envelopes, clipboards, scrolls — a whole stationery shop, no folders. Who makes an icon pack without a folder? Rejected the lot.

So I made my own. Pasted in a folder icon I liked, traced it into a duotone SVG, then let the machine do the retinting — navy and grey out, the app's palette in. The red chocolate and the mango shade, colors I named months ago and still stand behind. This is the agentic thing working the way it's supposed to: I point, it paints, I judge. Then the fun part: three variants — single folder, pair, stack — swapping them live with hot reload like trying on jackets. This is the part of the work I'd do for free. Change a line, watch the screen, feel it. The stack won immediately. A stack of folders says *cases* — plural, accumulating, work waiting inside. The single folder just said "folder." The pair said nothing at all.

Committed, pushed to the release branch. One icon, one commit, riding along in 1.13.0.

The physicist in me could run the numbers on this session and they would not be kind. Hours of effort, one small asset, a change maybe five users will consciously notice. But the ledger misses the point. I'll notice. Every time I open the home screen now, that button looks *right*, and the little tax I was paying on every launch is gone.

That's the whole entry. Some days you move the architecture. Some days you move an icon three pixels closer to what it was always supposed to be.
