Today's entire output: one icon.

The Cases button on the home screen had this book icon that never quite sat right with me. It said "library" when it should have said "folders." Small thing. The kind of small thing that's invisible to everyone else and mildly radioactive to me every time I open the app.

First stop, before drawing anything: dug through my icon packs on iCloud hoping someone had already made me a folder. Closest hits were an envelope, a clipboard, and a scroll. A scroll. For a folders button. Sometimes the ecosystem hands you exactly nothing and you just have to draw the thing yourself.

So I did. Pasted in a folder design I liked, traced it into a proper duotone SVG, then let the machine retint it out of its boring navy-and-grey into the app's palette — that red chocolate and mango combo that I'm still not tired of. This is the agentic loop working exactly how I want it: I point, it paints, I judge. No friction between "I want this" and "there it is."

Then the fun part: three variants side by side. Single folder, pair of folders, stack of folders. Hot reload on, swapping them live, squinting at the home screen like it's a wine tasting.

The stack won. It wasn't close, honestly. The single folder looked lonely. The pair looked accidental. The stack looks like *cases* — plural, accumulated, a thing you've been building. The icon finally says what the button does.

This is where my flow lives and I've stopped apologizing for it. No architecture today, no clever algorithms. Just an icon clicking into place on a screen. The dopamine is real and it's mine. And it came out better than anything I would have settled for from a pack.

Committed, pushed to the release branch. `cases_book.svg` is out, `folder_stack_duotone.svg` is in. One commit, one icon closer to 1.13.0.

Small day. Good day. The kind that doesn't make the highlight reel but makes the app feel a little more like it was made by someone who cared. That's the whole bet, really — that enough of these invisible days stack up into something visible. Like folders.
