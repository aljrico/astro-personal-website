Toast day.

Not the celebratory kind. The little-message-that-slides-in kind. The app had SnackBars scattered everywhere — hints, rewarded ads, purchase restores, the settings screen — and every single one of them needed a ScaffoldMessenger threaded through it like a hostage negotiation. Different tones, different timings, different vibes. Six flavors of "something happened" and none of them felt like they belonged to the same game.

So I built one toast. A single overlay, hosted at the root, with success and error and info variants and a little icon. Then I went hunting through the app replacing SnackBars one by one. Hint flow. Rewarded ad. Restore purchases. The level catalog. The Discord link and review prompt in settings. Each swap took thirty seconds and each one made the app feel slightly more like itself. This is the kind of work that doesn't demo well — nobody screenshots a toast — but you *feel* it. Consistency is invisible until it's missing.

Hot reload straight onto the actual iPhone the whole session, firing reload signals at a running process. Change, look, change, look. When the loop is that tight, UI work stops being engineering and becomes something closer to sculpting. This is the part of the job I'd do for free.

Also demoted a pill label from ALL CAPS to sentence case across every language. It was shouting. Nothing in this game should shout.

Tried a bolder title color while I was in there. Looked at it for a minute. Reverted. Some experiments exist just to confirm the current answer is right, and that's fine — cheaper than wondering.

By evening it all collapsed into one commit. A whole afternoon of tiny consistent decisions, flattened into a single line of history. The diff doesn't know how good the loop felt.

Then I closed the editor and opened the ad spend numbers — CPA caps, the least sculptural thing imaginable. Filed the analysis away in its own little bundle so future me can check whether the caps held. The physicist gets the evening shift. The artist got the afternoon. Fair trade, probably.
