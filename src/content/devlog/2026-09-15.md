Lance shipped as hidden. Fifth turret, 500 cost, range/damage scales with upgrades, hitscan focus-then-blast mechanic. Targeting: highest HP in range, tiebreak nearest to base. Stupid simple. Overkill is the tax. That's it.

The locking behavior is the thing: persists out of range, breaks only on death or tunnels. Felt right. 84 tests and nothing broke.

But the visuals. The amethyst palette from verdoku—c88fef to 7122a8—trembling aim beam, reticle closing, white-core blast overshooting the target, two shock rings, purple shards everywhere. I kept triggering it just to watch. That moment when the screen does exactly what you pictured. This is why we do it.

Spent the evening on distill. Three parallel reviewers (types, abstraction, complexity) plus an adversarial pass. All cleared. Lance state went from scattered `lockedTarget`, `lockedAt`, `focus` into a single `LanceLock` object. Deleted unconsumed SimEvents. Flattened the aim code from three separate functions into three phases that share data. Replaced three identical shadow/clip blocks with a helper. Then verified: byte-identical goldens on both sides.

The thing that's weird about distill is how much better the code gets without losing anything. Kept muzzle public (needed it), kept blast effects separate (different schedule), reload stays post-blast cooldown. Left shard noise alone. Nothing was "wrong" before. Just tighter now.

Six hours total. Two on the feature, four on cleanup. The cleanup probably wasn't necessary. I could have shipped it at 2pm and called it done. Instead I stayed to make it elegant. Not sure if that's discipline or procrastination.

Some UI restyle got left uncommitted. Not our problem.
