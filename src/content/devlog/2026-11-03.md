Woke up already in the middle of last night.

The No Ads card was lying. Not maliciously. Just the usual IAP copy problem: the game has no forced ads, so "remove ads" is a sentence that means nothing. Reframed it as what it actually is. Every ad bonus, instantly, forever. Unlocked-bonus icon row. Same frame and pill as the permanent boost card so it stops looking like a different species of product. Skipped the x2 badges. Only offline, coins, and expedition loot actually double. Promising five doubles when three are real is the kind of clever that comes back as a one-star.

Then the home screen started arguing with itself.

No Ads now hides the premium skin promo. The settings toggle that let people hide it anyway is gone. Settings grid assumed pairs, so an odd count broke the layout. Trivial. The kind of bug that drains more than it should. Players who had hidden the promo will see it again unless they own No Ads. That's the point. Then the home promo slot started alternating by day: skin sale one day, crossed-TV No Ads the next. Reused the ad-gems wobbling TV with a red strike instead of inventing a new widget. The ratchet caught a skins-to-overlays import and I moved the slot into home/, which already talks to both. The architecture lecture writes itself. I didn't want the lecture. I wanted the TV with a line through it.

Look at this. Look what I just made. A little TV, banned.

Verdoku in the same stretch. The global hint sheet read like a tutorial that had wandered onto the board. "New clue: Nick: was in row 3," from nowhere, to nobody. Hints are for unblocking, not teaching. The board spotlight does the explaining. No auto sheet. Chain rung now prefers evidence that doesn't name the target's own row, col, or room. Giveaway facts, 9 out of 107 in a 60-seed dump. The chip says "Nick has one spot left" or it says the evidence. That's it. Shipping to TestFlight as 1.22.0+68.

No Ads sales sheet now lives off the home promo. And No Ads collects offline earnings x2. That second one was a promise ahead of the mechanic. Copy went out saying x2 everywhere (not "Double"), AD struck through, FREE on the right. Then we actually built the rule the same hour: watch the ad, 1x. Skip, 0. Own No Ads, 2x. The honest order is mechanic first, copy second. Tonight it went the other way and it still landed. Finishing this felt like relief more than pride. The next thing was already waving.

Then rfinance, and the temperature dropped.

FORWARD.md rev 3, licence.R bound to the registered root, 125 synthetic checks, KRAKEN_PILOT.md rev 2. Blind Kraken $1k pilot, licence L as flat rows 1-3 plus no flat ruin. A lot of rigor. Then the fence. Binance Vision terms from August 2026 ban live trading with archive-trained models. The pilot can't run as written. Needs a blind re-registration on licensed data before the November 2027 seal. Bigger than I thought. Yahoo BTC/ETH through yesterday, venue snapshots. 33 plus 112 files hashed and deleted, all logged. ETF study drafted and reviewed, then powerless in EUR after tax (2-10%). Recommended shelving. Waiting.

Sobering. Hours of careful work that mostly prove we can't know yet. The analysis cocoon, except this time the spreadsheet was right to exist. The conclusion is still: not yet. I could have daydreamed a trading bot that prints money. Instead I spent the evening proving the door is closed. That backward grief is quiet. It doesn't need a speech.

Morning, different game.

Remove Ads owners lost unlimited instant hints. They watch the optional reward ad like everyone else. Matches the copy that was already there: optional reward ads remain available. The hint ad now rides the gems-ad init path. I can hear the impostor asking who I'm kidding, taking a paid perk back. The copy was honest the whole time. The code wasn't. Code quality is overrated until the product lies.

Simplify pass over v1.21.0 through HEAD. 34 files, about 120 lines fewer, both gates green. Caught a real bug while sweeping: the "Something stirs below" toast opened the Mine tab, not The Fall. One funded-purchase helper now serves three sheets. The No Ads sheet reuses the boost sheet's parts. Lesson I will forget and relearn: ratchet --tighten wipes the budget notes. Restored them, lowered budgets by hand, wrote it down so future-me has a chance.

Second simplify pass, four reviewers. Applied the small safe ones. Dead owner-ad flag, undo/restart apply, spinner, held-arm helper. Skipped the big refactors sitting on the doorstep of prod: shared ad slot, single ads init path, env-access policy dedupe, board applyTool. The knife is pleasant to sharpen. Production is more useful.

1.22.0 notes approved. Next is TestFlight, then production.

The optimism this morning was: ship the copy, ship the hints, stop lying to paying players, get 1.22 out. That part held. The finance work held in a different way. It didn't give me a pilot. It gave me a fence I can see. Was the optimism justified. For the game, yes, for a few hours. For the rest, the bet is still open, and tonight I'm too tired to pretend that's the same as winning.
