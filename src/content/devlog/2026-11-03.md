Woke up already in ship mode. Last night I rewrote a purchase around one sentence. This morning I made the rest of the product catch up.

No Ads owners had unlimited instant hints. A quiet little gift the card never mentioned. The copy has always said optional reward ads stay available. Today the code agrees. They watch the same hint ad as everyone else.

I sat with that for a minute. Some of them will feel it. I would. It still felt clean. A bonus the storefront did not sell is a bonus I should not keep.

Then a simplify pass over everything since 1.21.0. Thirty-four files. About a hundred and twenty lines gone. Both gates green. In the pile, a real bug: the "Something stirs below" toast opened the Mine tab instead of The Fall. Joder. Sitting there, waiting for someone to tap it at the worst possible moment.

One helper now feeds three paywalls. The No Ads sheet wears the boost sheet's parts. That is the lazy I actually like.

Ratchet --tighten ate the budget notes again. Restored them, lowered the numbers by hand, left a warning for next time. Second pass, four reviewers, a few minutes. The small cuts were obvious. Dead flag, spinner, undo. The big ones (shared ad slot, single init path, the board tool cleanup) I left on the table. Speed would have steamrolled them, and we are going to production today.

Store notes in twelve locales. Swipe-mark, hold Undo, new hints, pause curtain, Dead Cove, analytics opt-out, battery. Caught Islands in the approved copy. The game calls it Dead Cove. Fixed it before anyone else could.

Pre-created the 1.22.0 version so Game Center would attach itself. Then ship-prod died at submit. The CLI renamed --build to --build-id. Of course it did. Submitted by hand, ran the Android stage alone, fixed the script after. Temporarily shoved someone's drafts out of the tree to get past the clean check, put them back. iOS waiting for review. Play production done. Tagged.

The version is out. The feeling lasted about twenty minutes.

Last night was the fun part.

This game has no forced ads. So No Ads was always a strange thing to sell. The reframe landed and then I could not unsee it: every ad bonus, instantly, forever. Unlocked-bonus icons in a row. Same frame and pill as the permanent boost. Scattered cookies gone. I almost stamped x2 on every icon and then counted. Only offline, coins, and expedition loot actually double. Three out of five. The badges stayed off.

The promise line needed an outline just to read. Usually that is the padding fight that eats an afternoon. This time the type sat down and behaved.

Look at the home slot though. One day a skin sale. The next, the wobbling gem-ad TV with a red strike through it. Same widget. Day parity. The ratchet complained about a skins import, so I moved the slot into home, which already speaks both. I keep flipping the date in my head just to see the crossed TV again. Showed it to anyone nearby. They were polite. I was delighted enough for the room.

No Ads owners lose the premium skin promo. The settings grid assumed everything came in pairs until it did not. People who hid the promo will see it again unless they bought No Ads. Intended. A little rude. Fine.

Offline x2 was sitting in the copy ahead of the mechanic. I picked the rule in one pass: watch is 1x, skip is 0, No Ads is 2x. Built it the same hour. Copy live in the same breath. x2 everywhere. AD struck through. FREE on the right. Fast, a little messy, done.

Verdoku in the gaps. The global hint sheet felt like a tutorial sneaking in. "New clue: Nick was in row 3," from nowhere. We already have a board spotlight. Hints exist to unblock you when you are stuck. The sheet was teaching. I killed the auto sheet.

The chain now reaches for evidence that does not name the target's own row, column, or room. In a sixty-seed dump the giveaways were obvious once I looked. The chip can say "Nick has one spot left," or it can say the evidence. That is the whole hint. Sent it to TestFlight before bed.

And then the other pile, late.

Forward doc, licence, a hundred and twenty-five synthetic checks, all very serious. Then the venue terms from August. Archive-trained models cannot trade live. The blind pilot, as written, is dead. Needs a fresh registration on licensed data before the 2027 seal.

The fence was worse than I wanted it to be. Price history, venue snapshots, files that should not have been there. Hashed, deleted, logged. An ETF writeup that looks smart until you take tax in euros and you are left with two to ten percent. I recommended the shelf.

A lot of careful work whose main result is: we do not know yet. It felt productive. The awareness always arrives after. I could have closed the laptop at the terms page. I kept going until the deletions were done, which is probably the part that actually mattered.

The card is honest. The hints shut up. The crossed TV still makes me grin. Then I looked at the list of things that are still wrong, which is how you know a ship actually happened, and I opened the next thing.

The Yokai sheet. Fast loop. Tweak, hot reload, stare at it. Blood pips became a dashed feeding bar, labelled per feeding with the real effect. Ittan-Momen included. Then juice it. Overshoot fill, haptic ticks, squash, flash, shine, blood splash, a Yokai that breathes. I killed the red glow because it shoved the layout around. Juice with transforms only. Learned that the expensive way, in about four minutes.

The Fall lost its blood counter and its reset card. The refund card and the blackout files are sitting there orphaned. Decision pending, which in this house means postponed eternally.

Per-Yokai reset on the sheet. Five gems. Blocked while a fed child depends on it. Colour took three passes. Saturated gem purple was ugly. Ended on muted lilacs so the gem icon actually pops. The gate caught a convention test grepping for a literal button style. Widened it. The kind of fix that feels like cheating and also like the only sane move.

Then Tentatively. Daily panel rebuilt on Verdoku's layout: title lockup, global board, paper PLAY pill with a two-tone flag. Today card gone. Debug was blanking the panel because a sliver probe fought the board's layout builder. Ported Friends through a subagent, then dropped Friends and the difficulty selector, then reverted the native plumbing rather than ship it unused. Some back and forth on how exact the copy had to be. Moved the app to its own simulator after clobbering the other one. Venga.

Cookie last. Then Cookie again. Then Cookie until the afternoon ran out of names for it.

The wealth counter ticks at ten hertz and it stuttered. Tried Verdoku's digit roll first. Looked terrible. Then a vanilla numeric text library. Jumpy. Both restart the roll on every change, which is the whole problem when the number never sits still. The fix was velocity-preserving retargeting on per-glyph springs, not a new library. Three hundred and fifty-five tests green. Seven lines over the kit budget. Fine.

Skip pricing next. Garden and Mine now share one curve: minutes to the power of 0.8. The garden's old 1.5 was absurd. A three-hour skip went from two thousand four hundred gems to sixty-four. That is not a balance pass. That is admitting the previous number was a dare. Fixtures baked the prices in, so I had to inject the function or the parity checks would have died with the old curve. Tiny colour nudges on the pill until the tint sat. Disabled Send went red so it cannot pretend to be skip.

Then the footer. Four tabs again: Cookie, Buildings, Upgrades, Map. The map is Verdoku's minigame map wearing Cookie's clothes. Reveal is half the Farm's base price against lifetime cookies, the same rule the Farm already uses on itself, so I did not invent a latch. Named the key after the menu, because the old minigames highlight key is a ghost that gets pruned. Shared the tree with another session doing expeditions. Committed only my files and still managed to clobber the budget lines a few minutes later. Restored them. The show must go on.

Smith, Crystaller, Hollows got progress locks. First pickaxe costs ten ore. Ember got an icon and an x/10 pill, and the embers fly instead of toasting. Currency flights now ask where they are going every frame, so a pill that just appeared can still catch them. Another session had map work in the tree, so I gated mine into a scratch worktree and stayed out of their way.

The map itself was tiles that looked like homework until they did not. Restyled as Verdoku's level tiles, but squircles. Look at this. Look what I just made. Tiles from sixty-four to eighty-six. Art at two thirds. Doodles rasterized from the SVGs, scattered with rejection sampling, new seed every time you open it. Missed a colour-literal ratchet on the first push. Raised it six and left a note. The kind of note that is really an apology to future me.

Ready dots after that. Garden ripe, expedition done, Hollows run done. Smith dropped out of the plan: upgrades are instant in the port, so there is nothing to wait for. Reused the leaderboards red dot, moved it into the kit, gave it a popping spring. Hot reload choked on the new constructor fields. Hot restart fixed it. The other session was driving the sim too, so I stopped poking it. Some things you leave alone because the alternative is two people fighting over the same pixels.

Then the inner pages. Garden, Expeditions, Dough Jones. Each one a full page now. The footer stays on the main tabs. It was coming with you into Garden and it looked wrong.

The cookie button is Tiny Tiles. Same size, same spot as the original. I moved it into the currency row so the pills sit beside it instead of under it. Gems, coins, blood. It sat down like it had always been there.

The squircle button went into the kit. Same instinct as the paywall helper this morning.

The gate went red on bugs that were not mine. A raw random on the map page. A footer dispose that spawned a ticker. Already broken on the branch, so not even my mess. I was going to leave it. I did not. Untitled tiles on the unlocked map, ticker gone, random gone, gate green.

Bonus catch: the Ascension cloud bar has been a one-colour gradient throwing asserts since the port. Sitting there. Waiting. Same as the toast this morning. Disarmed it.

Then Tentatively again, because of course.

The home hero still said PLAY even when you were in the middle of a level. Under it, an unfinished card with a board preview, a timer, Discard. A whole object asking to be dealt with. I deleted it.

PLAY became a white CONTINUE LVL X pill. Only when a level is actually waiting. Fresh players start from Campsites, the way they should have all along.

Hot reload on the dedicated sim. Brisk. The kind of afternoon where the UI finally behaves and you do not ask why.

The game got more honest today. The trading stack got a closed door. The Yokai breathes. The map finally looks like a place you would tap. Tentatively's home screen got quieter. All of those can sit in the same day.
