Afternoon. Fonts and colors came first. Swapped everything over to TeX Gyre Heros — body, titles, menu buttons, page display. Nunito was fine. Heros reads cleaner, lands harder. Made a spreadsheet decision about which variant to use. Felt like it took fifty minutes. It took five. (Commit 08f0083.)

Campaign cards were muddy. Desaturated ground, grey backdrop fighting the lenses, pointless palette dots, weak everything. Tried two directions: portrait cards with blurry lenses, or a journey. The user picked the first. Then played with it live, and said: *the journey version is so much better. we're shipping that one.* That was the moment. That one decision unlocked everything downstream.

The journey line became regions as stations alternating left-right down a white road. Each station a lens onto a postcard — Softwood at L6, Apricot Bay at L26 showing the coast, Moss Valley at L52 both walls. Hand-picked colors per region — #FFDD94 for Softwood's warmth, #92d2ea for Apricot's sky. Dashes in Play yellow filling as clears pile up toward the next unlock. One full day of iteration collapsed into the right answer once we asked the right question: *why are we showing all the regions at once?* (Commits 08f0083, b37243e; 16:40.)

Home page got rebuilt. Dropped the ARCO wordmark, dropped the art demo, built a Mini Motorways city grid instead. TeX Gyre Heros again for "Mini TD." wordmark. Two streets — diagonal and cross — drawn static. Then invaders walk routes at in-game pace via a Ticker on a ValueNotifier. First cut had a `late final` ticker never accessed, nothing moved. Stared at it for two minutes before catching it. Tiny bug, total blocker. Switched the wordmark font to Chakra Petch Bold and the grid came alive. (Commit 7847864, 16:59.)

The invaders themselves went through vocabulary first. Started with a swimming outline, travelling wave plus a surge, one centered eye. Tried motes as drops, darts as long protozoans, tanks as superellipses. *Swimmers vs critters* — the vocabulary matters. Landed on Critters, then let them evolve. Amoebas with pseudopods, spirilla with corkscrew breathing. Rejected: bells, run-and-tumble rods, flocking specks, bead chains. Final roster: tadpoles (motes), water fleas (swarms), mantas (tanks), and colonies that burst into six real darts on impact. (Commits 5ba0eb0 18:45, eb6bfa3, 6bb5a4d 16:27.) The invader body drawing is shared now between home and game, so they're always in sync.

Field Guide got a second district: Turrets tab. Each tower runs on a real board, zoomed to a 2.6-cell window, with matched prey circling. Lance renamed to Lens. Dark rounded tile with a white-hot glass circle — 9 lilac motes accelerate in from 1.5 cells while focusing. The charging effect was tricky. Tried a black-hole warp first; the user said too aggressive. Switched to a light drain (75% desaturate, 22% dim) plus a subtle heat shimmer via fragment shader. The user wanted harder. Walked every knob back to the original. First instinct was right. (Commit 0aa6925, 13:29.)

Afternoon: the region selector got rebuilt as a scrolling level grid. Each region a station, 25 stops below it, next region at bottom. Per-region meander via seeded sine mix — Softwood gentle, Apricot Bay sweeps, Moss Valley switchbacks. Catmull-Rom curves, stops snapped to grid for fast renders. Locked levels greyed and blurred, baked into a snapshot. Maps built one per frame, next level prioritized so the visible region always renders first. Rare hitch if campaign opens within 500ms of launch, invisible on device. (Commits 13:03, 16:33, 16:34.)

Tried offscreen renders for screenshots. AnimatedSwitcher default Stack uses loose constraints — childless children collapse to zero size. Lenses empty. Spent an hour debugging that before finding it was the layoutBuilder. Swapped to StackFit.expand and it clicked.

Parallel thread: canvas sandbox for the in-game map redesign. Started on a separate worktree with four browser concepts (Canal Town, Specimen, Survey Sheet, Lights Out), landed on "way more minimal." Canvas on home grey grid. Black sea at the top, cream canal feeding into a white lake. Critters invisible in the sea, eyes fade in near shore. Tower picker as boxes (name set into the top edge, icon, price, reach preview). Reagent counter ticks up as critters die; homes counter jolts when lives are lost. (Worktree sandbox/canal, commits 17:20 onwards.)

Leak anim: critter collapses into its eye, eye arcs to the HUD. Shot critters: body bursts with spinning chunks and a white flash ring; shards fly in tower color. Those two motions looked similar enough to confuse what felt wrong. The user said "shake vs jump are different" — asked which motion when a counter felt off. Jolt on reagent gains got cut. Homes counter keeps the spring (lost life = physical hit, gain = calm). (Commits 18:59, 19:10.)

Growth animations: tried a ball-headed black front, rejected. Measured footage — roads swell from thin thread to full width, exponential ease. Final: rivers open pure charcoal with cream fade where they meet the lake, cream climbs back to the sea's head. The user cut screen shake, cracks, clods. Keep it calm. (Commits 18:55 onwards.)

Warning line across the canal: watermelon red dashed, snapped to grid. Critters past it blink red at 4Hz, still targetable. Red "clearly signals it reached destination." (Commit 19:10.)

Drag-to-build ghost floats above the thumb, snaps to grid, watermelon when invalid. Reach preview = hairline ring in tower shade color, water inside lit white. Holding Upgrade peeks the next rank's reach dashed. (Commit 19:27.)

Board repainted mid-afternoon in promo icon colors — ground #CBE2B5 grass, canal #F8F7EF cream, grid #D6E9C4. The user: "astonishingly good." Lake same cream as canal, no fade. (Commit 17:35.)

Wakes flipped to GameInk.enemy — full ink via saveLayer at 30% alpha, pencil feel. Ice trail GameInk.sky. Reach light-up changed from white overlay to tower color tint (since white on cream does nothing). (Commits 17:35, 17:40.)

Balance work ran parallel. CMA-ES bot was stuck in needle-only strategy. Ditched learned weights, ran a cheap beam search instead — find the minimal viable defense across waves 1 to 11, no genes, no evolution. Result: Needle + Ember, nothing fancy. Boring answer, clean answer. Bloom 0%, Frost 0%, Lance 0%. Just the two. That unblocked everything.

Starting cash 250. Ember 250 cost, dmg 7/11/16. Bloom 250, reload 2/1.5/1, dmg 3/6/12, range 3/4/4.5. The user's call: buff Bloom's range identity, not DPS parity. Range alone moved Bloom from 3% to 12% of bot spending. Damage buff alone did nothing.

Waves as floor formulas. Difficulty starts at 0, climbs with level. Bounty = floor(5 + 2√hp). That's the real tuning lever. Hits-to-kill matters more than raw HP. A fractional multiplier had quietly given darts extra hits; floors fixed it.

Six dailies passed bit-identical fingerprints. TestFlight builds stacked up: 14, 15, 17, 19, 20, 21, 23, 25, 26, 29, 30, 31, 32. Each one a reason — palette, unlock gate, winding road, analytics wired. (Build 32 commit 2a73313.)

Signed build 32, went to ship it. Xcode's Apple ID token was dead. Just fails with "No Accounts". Tried the workaround — ASC API key, `-authenticationKeyPath`, issuer from config. It worked but it's friction we shouldn't carry. Someone will hit this again. Note for next time: remove + re-add Apple ID in Xcode settings.

Good momentum on the UI and tuning. Build shipped clean. Infrastructure pain at the finish line, usual story. Tomorrow: verify the balance holds, then think about what's next.
