CMA-ES bot was stuck in a local optimum. So we ditched learned weights entirely and ran a cheap beam search instead—find the minimal viable defense across waves 1 to 11, no genes, no evolution. Result: Needle + Ember, nothing fancy. Boring answer, but clean. It means everything else actually matters instead of being noise around the dominant strategy.

That unblocked the balance work we'd been holding. But the day sprawled. Campaign redesign landed—regions as stations along a journey line, each one a postcard lens into the board. Softwood first, then Apricot Bay with its shoreline, Moss Valley with its canyon walls, Rust Mesa with tunnels. Twenty-five levels per region, hand-authored. Invaders got redrawn as soft swimmers—tadpoles, water fleas, mantas, colonies that split into darts. The Field Guide got a Turrets tab showing each tower on a real board, magnified in a 2.6-cell window.

Multiple TestFlight builds shipped as each piece landed. 14, 15, 17, 19-26, 29, 30, 31, 32. Each one was a reason—palette, unlock gate, winding road, analytics wired. The notes are full of small iterations. Half-cell erosion on canyon walls. Coral headlands for the bay. Shader warps for the Lens tower, then reverts when they felt wrong. Reagent counters that tick instead of jump. A warning line on the canal when rivers start to grow.

The bot work ran parallel. Beam search told us Needle + Ember. But evolved bots didn't match hand-tuned bots, and evolved bots stuck in local optima. Rounds of genes. Pools and isolates. Clamping helpers. Bit-identical fingerprints across 536 validation games. Perf debates. The user rejected certain balance changes—nerfing Bloom, for example—so we iterated instead. The real move came down to one insight: Bloom's range buff instead of DPS. Range alone moved it from 3% to 12% of bot spending, whereas damage tweaks alone did nothing. Small lever, right place. User's instinct was right.

Pushed Build 32 to TestFlight, thought we were done. Then tried to upload and Xcode's stored Apple ID token died. Just fails with "No Accounts". There's a command-line workaround (ASC API key), but it's friction we shouldn't carry. It's a known problem—token expires, needs re-adding in Xcode settings. Has to be done manually. Someone will hit this again.

Good momentum on the tuning work itself. Build shipped clean. Infrastructure headaches at the finish line, usual story.
