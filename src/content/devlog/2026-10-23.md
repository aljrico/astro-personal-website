Morning started with fonts. Swapped everything to TeX Gyre Heros (GUST license) — body, titles, menu buttons, locked it in everywhere except the logo and a few legacy spots via commit 08f0083. Nunito was fine. Heros reads cleaner, lands harder. Later bundled Chakra Petch Bold (Google Fonts OFL) for the wordmark and square buttons (Campaign/Survival/Resume, commit 7847864) — that sci-fi feel almost clashed with the soft organic swimmers, but kept it anyway.

Campaign cards came next (commit b37243e). They were muddy — desaturated ground, grey backdrop fighting lenses, pointless palette dots, weak everything. Tried portrait cards with blurry lenses; user picked the journey direction instead. That unlock everything downstream. Colors iterated live: region ground #cfe8d5 (Moss Valley), #EED3B8 (Apricot Bay), #CBE2B5 (Softwood). Hand-picked pastels per region, bigger lens with soft white rim, new lighter backdrop, dots gone. Play tags (commit c915cb2) added flat #F8C66A with white text, bottom-right, matched a reference.

Home page rebuilt (commit ad73418). Dropped the ARCO wordmark, dropped the art demo, built a Mini Motorways city grid instead — ground #E5EBEA, faint grid, two wide white static streets. Invaders walk three routes at in-game pace via a Ticker. First cut had a `late final` ticker never accessed so nothing moved; caught it, fixed it. Added the wordmark in Chakra Petch Bold and the grid came alive.

Invaders went through iterations. Started with a swimming outline (commit 5ba0eb0) — travelling wave plus surge, one centered eye. Named them: mote (tadpole), dart (water flea hop & drift), tank (manta), colony (6 darts). Vocabulary mattered; rejected spirillum, bells, runs. By 18:45 (commit 6b6045c), the roster locked as one dart family — colony now bursts into 6 real darts instead of motes; waveWeight in sixths, unpaid.

TestFlight 1.1.0 shipped early (builds 14, 15) to test the new roster before the big push. The invader body drawing is shared now between home and game so they're always in sync.

Field Guide got a second district: Turrets tab (commit 0aa6925). Each tower runs on a real board zoomed to a 2.6-cell window, with matched prey circling. Lance renamed to Lens (commit 0aa6925). Dark rounded tile with a white-hot glass circle — 9 lilac motes accelerate in from 1.5 cells while focusing. Charging effect was tricky. Tried a black-hole warp first; rejected as too aggressive. Switched to a light drain via fragment shader (75% desaturate, 22% dim) plus subtle heat shimmer. User wanted harder; walked every knob back to the original. First instinct was right. Then added tower toast (commit f757915): after a win's stars land on the end card, a dark card drops from the top with "FROST UNLOCKED" or the next tower dimmed with star progress and a filling bar. Small moment, huge juice. Simplify pass (commit 9b14861) memoized the arenas across tab switches, cut arena map gen from 34–73ms per toggle.

Afternoon: the region selector got rebuilt as a winding road (commit 152a3e1). Each region a station, 25 stops, next region at bottom. Per-region meander via seeded sine mix — Softwood gentle, Apricot Bay long sweeps, Moss Valley switchbacks. Catmull-Rom curves, stops snapped to grid for fast renders. Locked levels greyed and blurred, baked into a snapshot. Maps built one per frame, next level prioritized so the visible region always renders first. Rare hitch if campaign opens within 500ms of launch, invisible on device. Bump to build 29 (commit 0cfef58) shipped the winding-road level selector. Simplify pass (commit 663d0bd) moved map builds to an Isolate, measured ~150-200ms AOT cost, cut it by deferring off the main thread. Perf agent was right; Codex's guess (sim hot loops) was wrong.

Parallel thread: canvas sandbox for the in-game map redesign on a separate worktree (sandbox/canal). Started with four browser concepts, landed on "way more minimal." Canvas on home grey grid. Black sea at the top, cream canal feeding into a white lake. Critters invisible in the sea, eyes fade in near shore. Tower picker as boxes (name set into the top edge, icon, price, reach preview via a tinted circle). Reagent counter ticks up as critters die; homes counter jolts when lives are lost. Leak anim: critter collapses into its eye, eye arcs to the HUD. Shot critters: body bursts with spinning chunks and a white flash ring; shards fly in tower color. User said "shake vs jump are different" — asked which motion when a counter felt off. Jolt on reagent gains got cut (commit 0612ab4). Homes counter keeps the spring.

Board repainted mid-afternoon (commit e8f93da) in promo icon colors — ground #CBE2B5 grass, canal #F8F7EF cream, grid #D6E9C4. User: "astonishingly good." Lake same cream as canal, no fade. Wakes flipped to full ink via saveLayer at 30% alpha, pencil feel. Ice trail GameInk.sky. Reach light-up changed from white overlay to tower color tint (since white on cream does nothing). Shot critters now mirror the leak anim: collapse into eye, charcoal eye arcs to reagent counter (commit 5513d8b). Bounty impact: burst + jolt + flash in Play yellow, softer than the red life-lost hit. After a win's stars land on the end card, a dark card drops: "FROST UNLOCKED" or the next tower dimmed with star progress and a filling bar. Tower unlock became visual juice.

Regional work branched in parallel. Softwood and other regions carved out onto the canvas board. Palette decisions locked per region. Apricot Bay: user proposed ground #EED3B8, grid #F1DCC6 (sampled from Mini Motorways reference). Moss Valley: #cfe8d5 ground, canyon carved via seeded sine meander. Rust Mesa: organically grown mesa placed over the first river so it tunnels 3–8 cells hidden. By 18:09, the campaign order was locked: Moss Valley (easiest by definition), Apricot Bay, Softwood, Rust Mesa. All 100 levels seeded (commit 2a73313), 25 per region, unique palette, terrain rules, and difficulty curve per region.

Tower earning system came next (commit 19b3e16). Towers unlocked by campaign stars: Needle+Bloom free, Frost 25★, Ember 50★, Lance 100★. Dailies use the player's own unlocks. Team rejected per-critter tier rings (too noisy), kept tank bar flattened to 1px corners with a faint track.

Balance work hit a wall and then a breakthrough. CMA-ES bot was stuck in needle-only strategy. Ditched learned weights, ran a cheap beam search instead (tool/search.dart, commit 9bff353) — find the minimal viable defense across waves 1 to 11, no genes, no evolution. Result: Needle + Ember, nothing fancy. Boring answer, clean answer. Bloom 0%, Frost 0%, Lance 0%. Just the two.

That unblocked everything. Starting cash 250 (commit 818a51e). Needle reload/dmg locked. Ember 250 cost, dmg 7/11/16. Bloom 250, reload 2/1.5/1, dmg 3/6/12, range 3/4/4.5. User's call: buff Bloom's range identity, not DPS parity. Range buff alone moved Bloom from 3% to 12% of bot spending. Damage buff alone did nothing. The insight: specialist towers matter when they solve what others can't, not when they out-DPS the generalist.

Waves rewritten as floor formulas (commits 1d384aa, 818a51e). Difficulty starts at 0.01·(level−1), climbs per region. Bounty = floor(5+2√hp). Hits-to-kill matters more than raw HP. A fractional multiplier had quietly given darts extra hits; floors fixed it. Multiple balance rounds followed — round 1 (shells, splash, chill stacks), round 2 (wave-mix genes, hybrid seeding), round 3 (too easy, killed), round 4 (Ember overtuned), round 5 (Ember 7/11/16, Bloom 3/6/12). Each round tightened the knob. Final wave spec locked: Frost .2/.3/.5 range 1.5/1.75/2 with stacking chill; Bloom 3/6/12; Ember 7/11/16; Needle 1/1.15/1.3.

Six dailies passed bit-identical fingerprints across balance iterations. TestFlight builds stacked up: 14, 15, 17, 19, 20, 21, 23, 25, 26, 29, 30, 31, 32. Each one a reason — palette, unlock gate, winding road, analytics wired, regional rework, balance round, UI simplification pass, towers earned, Field Guide turrets.

Signed build 32 (commit at 19:35), went to ship it. Xcode's Apple ID token was dead. Just fails with "No Accounts". Tried the workaround — ASC API key, `-authenticationKeyPath`, issuer from config. It worked but it's friction we shouldn't carry. Root fix: remove + re-add the Apple ID in Xcode settings → Accounts, then the stored token refreshes. Someone will hit this again.

Good momentum on the UI and tuning. Build shipped clean. Infrastructure pain at the finish line, usual story. The beam search insight unlocked the whole balance question — minimal towers, maximum elegance. Tomorrow: verify the balance holds on device, then think about what's next. Maybe the currency system for Ember unlock, maybe the regional tweaks. Or maybe just rest.
