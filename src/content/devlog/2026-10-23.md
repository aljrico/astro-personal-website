Morning was fonts and colors. Swapped everything over to TeX Gyre Heros—body, titles, menu buttons. The old Nunito was fine. Heros reads cleaner, lands harder. Made a spreadsheet decision that took five minutes and felt like it took fifty.

Campaign cards needed rescuing. They were muddy. Desaturated ground, grey backdrop fighting the lenses, pointless palette dots, weak everything. Tried two directions: portrait cards with blurry lenses, or a journey. User picked the first. Then played with it live, and said: *the journey version is so much better. we're shipping that one.* Listened for that exact moment. That one decision unlocked everything downstream.

The journey line became regions as stations alternating left-right down a white road. Each station a lens onto a postcard (Softwood at L6, Apricot Bay at L26 showing the coast, Moss Valley at L52 both walls). Hand-picked colors per region—#FFDD94 for the warmth, #92d2ea for sky. Dashes in Play yellow filling as clears pile up toward the next unlock. One full day of iteration collapsed into the right answer once we asked the right question: *why are we showing all the regions at once?*

Home page got rebuilt too. Dropped the ARCO wordmark, dropped the art demo, built a Mini Motorways city grid instead. TeX Gyre Heros again for "Mini TD." wordmark (full stop in Play yellow). Two streets—diagonal and cross—drawn static. Then invaders walk routes at in-game pace via a Ticker on a ValueNotifier. First cut had a `late final` ticker never accessed, nothing moved. Stared at it for two minutes before catching it. Tiny bug, total blocker. Switched the wordmark font to Chakra Petch Bold and the grid came alive.

The invaders themselves went through revisions. Started with a swimming outline, travelling wave plus a surge, one centered eye. Tried motes as drops, darts as long protozoans, tanks as superellipses. Colony was five pulsing members. User's vocabulary check: "swimmers" vs "critters"—the vocabulary matters. Settled on Critters. Then the roster kept shifting—amoebas with pseudopods, spirilla with corkscrew breathing. By the end: tadpoles, water fleas, mantas, and colonies that burst into six real darts on impact. The invader body drawing is shared now between home and game, so they're always in sync.

Field Guide got a second district: Turrets tab. Each tower runs on a real board, zoomed to a 2.6-cell window, with matched prey circling. Lance renamed to Lens. Dark rounded tile with a white-hot glass circle—9 lilac motes accelerate in from 1.5 cells while focusing. The charging effect was tricky. Tried a black-hole warp first; user said too aggressive. Switched to a light drain (75% desaturate, 22% dim) plus a subtle heat shimmer via a fragment shader. User wanted harder. Walked every knob back to the original. First instinct was right.

Afternoon: the region selector got rebuilt as a scrolling level grid. Each region a station, 25 stops below it, next region at bottom. Per-region meander via seeded sine mix—Softwood gentle, Apricot Bay sweeps, Moss Valley switchbacks. Catmull-Rom curves, stops snapped to grid for fast renders. Locked levels greyed and blurred, baked into a snapshot. Maps built one per frame, next level prioritized so the visible region always renders first. Rare hitch if campaign opens within 500ms of launch, invisible on device.

Tried offscreen renders for screenshots. AnimatedSwitcher default Stack uses loose constraints—childless children collapse to zero size. Lenses empty. Spent an hour debugging that before finding it was the layoutBuilder. Swapped to StackFit.expand and it clicked.

Parallel thread: canvas sandbox for the in-game map redesign. Started with four browser concepts (Canal Town, Specimen, Survey Sheet, Lights Out), landed on "way more minimal." Canvas on home grey grid. Black sea at the top, cream canal feeding into a white lake. Critters invisible in the sea, eyes fade in near shore. Tower picker as boxes (name set into the top edge, icon, price, reach preview). Reagent counter ticks up as critters die; homes counter jolts when lives are lost.

Leak anim: critter collapses into its eye, eye arcs to the HUD. Shot critters: body bursts with spinning chunks and a white flash ring; shards fly in tower color. Those two motions looked similar enough to confuse what felt wrong. User said "shake vs jump are different"—asked which motion when a counter felt off. Jolt on reagent gains got cut. Homes counter keeps the spring (lost life = physical hit, gain = calm).

Growth animations: tried a ball-headed black front, rejected. Measured footage—roads swell from thin thread to full width, exponential ease. Final: rivers open pure charcoal with cream fade where they meet the lake, cream climbs back to the sea's head. User cut screen shake, cracks, clods. Keep it calm.

Warning line across the canal: watermelon red dashed, snapped to grid. Critters past it blink red at 4Hz, still targetable. Red "clearly signals it reached destination."

Drag-to-build ghost floats above the thumb, snaps to grid, watermelon when invalid. Reach preview = hairline ring in tower shade color, water inside lit white. Holding Upgrade peeks the next rank's reach dashed.

Board repainted mid-afternoon in promo icon colors—ground #CBE2B5 grass, canal #F8F7EF cream, grid #D6E9C4. User: "astonishingly good." Lake same cream as canal, no fade.

Wakes flipped to GameInk.enemy—full ink via saveLayer at 30% alpha, pencil feel. Ice trail GameInk.sky. Reach light-up changed from white overlay to tower color tint (since white on cream does nothing).

Balance work ran parallel. CMA-ES bot was stuck. Ditched learned weights, ran a cheap beam search instead—find the minimal viable defense across waves 1 to 11, no genes, no evolution. Result: Needle + Ember, nothing fancy. Boring answer, clean answer. Bloom 0%, Frost 0%, Lance 0%. Just the two.

That unblocked everything. Starting cash 250. Ember 250 cost, dmg 7/11/16. Bloom 250, reload 2/1.5/1, dmg 3/6/12, range 3/4/4.5. User's call: buff Bloom's range identity, not DPS parity. Range alone moved Bloom from 3% to 12% of bot spending. Damage buff alone did nothing.

Waves as floor formulas. Difficulty starts at 0, climbs with level. Bounty = floor(5 + 2√hp). That's the real tuning lever. Hits-to-kill matters more than raw HP. A fractional multiplier had quietly given darts extra hits; floors fixed it.

Six dailies passed bit-identical fingerprints. TestFlight builds stacked up: 14, 15, 17, 19, 20, 21, 23, 25, 26, 29, 30, 31, 32. Each one a reason—palette, unlock gate, winding road, analytics wired.

Signed build 32, went to ship it. Xcode's Apple ID token was dead. Just fails with "No Accounts". Tried the workaround—ASC API key, `-authenticationKeyPath`, issuer from config. It worked but it's friction we shouldn't carry. Someone will hit this again. Note for next time: remove + re-add Apple ID in Xcode settings.

Good momentum on the UI and tuning. Build shipped clean. Infrastructure pain at the finish line, usual story. Tomorrow: verify the balance holds, then think about what's next.
