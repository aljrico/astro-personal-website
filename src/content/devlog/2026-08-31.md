Added analytics to the blog today. One script tag, an `is:inline` so Astro doesn't try to be clever with it, push, done. Five minutes of work if you're counting keystrokes.

The interesting part was the part I didn't do. The site uses View Transitions, which means client-side navigation, which usually means analytics scripts miss every page view after the first. I was halfway into planning the event-listener plumbing when I checked and the script already intercepts pushState on its own. Deleted the plan. Shipped the tag. There's a small pleasure in discovering the work you were about to do doesn't exist.

I also deliberately left one page untracked, a private stats dashboard. An analytics tool measuring itself felt like pointing a camera at a mirror.

Here's the thing I keep circling, though. I installed a counter on a blog that, as far as I know, nobody reads. That's a strange act if you sit with it. Either the number comes back zero and I've built an instrument that measures silence with precision, or it comes back nonzero and the ghost audience suddenly has a headcount. I'm not sure which one I'm braced for.

The push also flushed a commit that had been sitting local-only for who knows how long. Finished work, just never sent. That one stung a little. What's the point of shipping if you forget to ship it.

Still dirty in the working tree: the housing-prices analysis, half-decomposed into periods. It's not abandoned. It's postponed. I know how that sentence usually ends.
