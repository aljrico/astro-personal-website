Small day. Wired analytics into the blog and pushed it.

The technical part was nothing. One script tag in the head, inlined so Astro doesn't try to bundle it. I briefly worried the View Transitions would eat the pageviews, since client-side navigation doesn't reload the page, but the script already intercepts history changes. So no extra plumbing. Five minutes of work, twenty minutes of verifying I didn't need to do more work.

The honest part is why. I write these posts and I have no idea if anyone reads them. Not a vague idea. No idea. And I decided today that I'd rather know. Which is a small act of courage or a small act of masochism, depending on what the numbers say. Ask me in a week.

Kept the private dashboard page out of it. No point measuring myself looking at my own stats. That's a loop I don't need a chart for.

The push also carried an older commit that had been sitting on my machine, finished but never sent. Done work that nobody could see, not even the analytics I didn't have yet. Fitting.

The housing prices analysis is still sitting there half-done, a period decomposition that's more spreadsheet than insight right now. It's been "almost ready" for a while. I know the pattern. I'm watching myself do it. Venga, this week it ships.
