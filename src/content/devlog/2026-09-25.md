Morning: the roads were wrong. Concentric circles at 0.70-cell radius pinched at inside corners, warped radii on short legs. The geometry didn't survive contact with reality. Burned it down and restarted.

Quarter circles instead. Fixed per cell, radius 0.5, one stroke per route via the new `roundedPolyline`. Cleaner on screen. Pushed it straight to main (carried an unpushed commit along with it).

Pulled the dead weight while I was in there: cornerRadii, roadOutline, filletPolyline, truncatePolyline, the junctions param. The code was already explaining itself—no need to keep the old sketches around.

Road width dropped from 0.55 to 0.45 cells. That's where the problem lives now. Enemy bodies overhang the narrower lanes—the super tank alone is 0.5 wide with no margin. Either shrink the bodies by about 20% or admit the old width was right. Tomorrow problem. The roads look better today and that mattered more.
