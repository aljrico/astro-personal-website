Morning was campaign cards. Region colours iterated live—Pacific Haze, lavender (rejected, clashed with warm palette), coral, poppy, landing on #FFDD94. User picked it immediately. That's how you know. Lens miniatures show the coast now, water visible, nothing clipped. Fixed the Apricot Bay bug where the inlet was drawing as a straight bar (preview wasn't passing shore paths). Two hours of invisible work that snapped into place.

Font swap everywhere: TeX Gyre Heros, because Helvetica doesn't bundle on Android and Dinosaur Polo Club isn't going to let us off easy. Added Chakra Petch Bold for the wordmark and buttons—techy feel that shouldn't work against the soft swimmers but somehow does. User kept it. Noted the trademark risk ("Mini TD" + MM reference = App Store trouble) but that's a future problem.

Home page rebuilt as a Mini Motorways city. Two white streets, a diagonal meeting a cross street, all right angles. Invaders walk the routes at game pace. Spent an hour figuring out why nothing was moving, then realized the ticker was never actually running (late final, never accessed). Once that was fixed, traffic moved. There's something good about watching little creatures walk through empty space before you've built anything.

Invader roster landed after a bunch of false starts. mote = tadpole, swarm = water flea, tank = manta, colony = six darts now instead of five motes. Amoebas, spirilla, krill, bells—all rejected. The switch matters for wave weight. Updated tests, updated help text. Haven't playtested late-wave Survival with the fast dart broods yet, but that's not today.

Shipped TestFlight 1.1.0 twice (first build failed, train was closed). Built from clean worktrees both times to keep user's WIP out. Four files are sitting uncommitted—inverted icons, review sheet tests, a store flow. They'll land when they're ready. Right now they'd just muddy the binary.

Then context switched to the canal.

Working in a sandbox worktree. The canal is a separate game mode: critters filling a waterway, trying to reach the lake before the water overflows. Browser concepts first (Canal Town, Specimen, Survey Sheet, Lights Out), then user said "way more minimal." Landed on: black sea at top, white lake at bottom, critters invisible in the sea until they surface near shore. No swell, no waves, no pulsing mouths. Just the outline.

Objective mechanics went through iterations. Houses filling with goo. Row of houses. Comb branches. Finally: last stretch darkens one-tenth per leak, liquid front crawls across the board, critters targetable until contact. Last stretch turns white, never darkens. Draining into clarity.

HUD was three rewrites. Cut level name, wave count, unit labels. Now it's just wave bar on top, one line below with homes/reagent/timer counters. Minimalist won.

Tower picker started as floating cards, became drag-to-build. Ghost floats above thumb, snaps to grid, red if invalid. Reach preview is a hairline ring in tower colour with water lit white inside (shows coverage). Tap-arm and tap-twice kept as fallback. Selecting a tower morphs the picker into three boxes: name and rank pips, UPGRADE with deltas, SELL. Board stays clear. User rejected the "yellow drop flies from box into cell" juice but kept counter ticking down and drop-in squash.

Animations were the long tail. Leak collapse: 0.3 seconds, body swells once and twists inward, wrapped in a red radial glow that gathers onto the eye, eye arcs to homes counter. Shot critters burst into seven spinning chunks plus white flash plus colour-matched shards, eye flies to reagent counter. Bounty impact same path but play yellow.

Reagent counter bit me twice. First it jolted and sprang on gains. User said no. Ripped it out. Then it still "jumped" because the digit swap animation restarted every tick. An AnimatedSwitcher detail nobody thinks about until it's wrong. Fixed: swap with Duration.zero. Homes counter keeps the drop-in slide because lost life is a hit; gain is just gain.

River growth came last. Three kinds: merge into a canal side, fork from a canal corner to the lake, independent new river. Eyes gather at the new mouth (user loved that). Growth anim is simple: charcoal with cream fade where it meets water, then cream climbs back up. User cut screen shake, ground cracks, clods, rings. Keep it calm. A bug: per-river warning line snapped to grid and landed high on the fork. Fixed by anchoring one board-wide line to the last full row's bottom.

Nine hours of iteration. Back and forth with user on small decisions. Palette shift at the very end made the whole system look astonishingly good. Grass green ground, cream canal and lake, darker grass grid. It locked in.

Opening tomorrow: home page still on grey grid, should move to the same palette. Haven't playtested canal flow yet. Night finale is deferred. User's WIP files waiting.

The work felt good. The iteration rhythm was right. User and I trading decisions, landing on small moments that worked. But I know there's more. There always is. And something in me wants to stay in this system and keep polishing the animations. Another part wants to move on.

It's past midnight. The second register is loud now. The question isn't whether the canal is good (it is). The question is whether this is the right thing to be spending time on, and I won't know the answer until I step back from it.
