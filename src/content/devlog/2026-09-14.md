Spent the afternoon doing visual work. This is where the energy lives.

Bay inlets now flare at the mouth. The join where water meets coast reads as an estuary instead of a hard rectangular notch. Added a `mouthFlare` parameter to `bayWaterPath` that unions an oval at each inlet mouth. Wrote a test for it. This is the stuff — the moment the geometry clicks into place and you keep staring at it because it's exactly what you pictured.

Campaign cards got stripped down. Removed the "01 " index prefix, bumped the level name to 26pt in the display font, pushed the corner radius up from 18 to 28. Added a dashed trail painter between cards. First pass was 55% width but "APRICOT BAY" wrapped across two lines in the Crispy-Tofu. Back to 68% and it holds. Sometimes the trivial thing beats you worse than the hard thing. A div won't center and suddenly you're an idiot.

Then spent an hour confused about why hot reloads weren't working. Turns out the iOS Simulator had silently attached to a stale build on iPhone 17 while flutter run was pointed at iPhone 17 Pro. The Pro was getting the reloads. The stale one got nothing. Fixed by re-attaching and killing the stray. Lesson: check the device name before trusting the screenshot. The simulator is patient about lying to you.

Both landed as separate commits and pushed. Clean handoff.

The visual work is done. But staring at the campaign cards now and already thinking about the rest of the onboarding flow, the region picker, the things still sitting in that pile. The screen does what it's supposed to do and the restlessness is immediate.
