Morning was clean. Wrapped Phase 4 consent-aware post-game review and ad precedence logic. The kind of work that feels solid while you're doing it — validation passing 119 tests, analyzer clean, no surprises in the final push. When an ad state is impossible, we keep retrying. When UMP readiness is memoized, we trust it. Wired the monetization purchase callback to the shared review engine and it all snapped together. That's the feeling you want: the pieces were always meant to fit, you just had to find the right angle.

Then the afternoon was different.

Pushed the roads refactor. Been staring at those cell-based curves for weeks — the ambitious approach was 0.70-cell radius with filled concentric outlines. Looked great in theory. On screen it fell apart. The radii would shrink on short legs, inside corners pinched, it felt cramped. All that complexity for a worse result.

Switched to something simpler. Fixed radius-0.5 quarter circles, one clean stroked centerline per route, junctions baked in. Ripped out the whole cornerRadii/filletPolyline/truncatePolyline machinery. Road width down to 0.45 cells. Smaller, cleaner, actually *better*. That moment — when you realize the elaborate solution was the wrong solution — never gets old. The irony is it feels like failure until you see it work.

There's an overhang problem now. Enemy bodies at 0.5 width don't fit the narrower road. Could shrink the bodies, could push the road back to 0.5. Open either way. Diagonal staircases are still a maybe for step 5.

One gotcha that made me laugh: left a test run idle overnight. Daily survival mode. Lost at 0:43. Posted to the actual leaderboard under a test account name. Real time, fake run, real ledger entry. Test on campaign levels going forward.

Bits still uncommitted — home button inversions, formatter churn, some store infrastructure scaffolding. They can wait. The roads are where the focus needed to be and they're better now for the simplification.

The day had weight to it. The kind of day where you ship something real and immediately see the problems that create, and that's fine. The game is getting denser. The visual decisions matter more now. You feel that.
