Plumbing morning.

Gitignored tentatively's local reports dir and pushed. Not a build, so v1.0.0 still lives on the last real commit. Then I wired RevenueCat to Google's real-time notifications. Topic generated, Play publisher granted on that topic only. I almost opened it to allUsers. Hostia. Caught it before it left my hands.

iOS went back in yesterday with the device video. Auto-release is on. Both stores are in review. Now I wait. I filled the wait with everything else on this list, which is how these days go.

Play trader status is next. DSA paperwork. The glamorous life.

Pulled ASA attribution out of Verdoku and into flama_analytics_flutter. Capture once, Flama-prefixed names so Verdoku's own types don't collide when the pin jumps. The kind of rename that feels like busywork until it isn't.

Paused events skip the upload without burning an attempt. Verdoku was sending them anyway. Empty install id gets checked before opt-out, so a failed store read doesn't freeze into a permanent no. I only notice these after living in the path too long.

Ran an SPM test because I wanted to be sure. Swift does not auto weak-link AdServices. No LC_LOAD_DYLIB. You need the explicit -weak_framework flag or you ship a crash on devices that don't have it. Glad I checked. I would have assumed the compiler was smarter than me.

Cookie got ad_paid from AdMob and the ASA install path through flama. Same prefs keys, so Verdoku can switch later without a migration story. Added it to the attribution allowlist.

That came out of the Apple Ads ROI dig. The campaign was roughly break-even. France, Germany, Australia, US made money. Spain, UK, Canada, Netherlands did not. I want per-campaign LTV before I turn spend back on. The physicist in me made a little table and felt useful. The other part of me knows I could restart the winners tomorrow and learn faster. For once I'm letting the table win. It's actual money.

Then I locked all six flama consumers to the new head. Lockfile-only, not even pushed. Cookie and conducir were stale for no reason. The projects already on main were silently a few commits behind through their locks. Drift you only see when you go looking.

Wrote a bump script. Upgrade, analyze, commit the lock. Skip dirty trees. Skip docs-only. Put a rule in the global instructions. Felt like I was building infrastructure. Felt responsible.

Twenty minutes later I deleted it.

Hardcoded project list. Every new game is another line to remember. A push model with extra steps. The pull model is pin to main and upgrade flama before you build. Zero maintenance. New games get it for free.

I knew this. I still wrote the script. Then I looked at it and the gut said no, and for once I listened before the thing grew roots.

The stores are still in review. The ads are still paused. The script is gone. The package moved. Was the optimism justified? Ask me when someone actually reviews the build.
