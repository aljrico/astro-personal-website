Two separate systems went live today. They don't quite know about each other yet.

Morning: pushed the road refactor. Exact arc math, quarter circles, 0.45-cell stroked lines, one centreline per route. The theory held up better than the filled concentric outlines, so I committed. Mini Motorways uses atlas tiles; we're faking it with math.

Then I saw what I'd missed. The narrower roads mean enemy bodies overhang. Super tank is 0.5 wide. Small tank was fine. This one isn't. Options are expensive—shrink bodies ~20% and the collision feel changes everywhere, or widen the roads and the layout tightens everywhere. Joder.

I made the commit knowing the picture was incomplete. This is the cost of moving fast. Decisions compound before you see them all together.

Evening was the monetization layer. Wired `onPurchased` to the shared `ReviewEngine`. Consent-aware post-game flow, memoized UMP readiness, 60-second no-stack guard, ads-impossible states retrying. Validation passed—119 green, 2 skipped, clean analyzer. Invisible plumbing. Works or breaks. Today it worked.

One moment of retroactive comedy: an idle daily survival run in the sim lost and posted to the real leaderboard. Test account, 0:43. Caught it hours later. The kind of thing that stings because you realize you weren't thinking carefully enough. Now testing on campaign levels instead.

Left some changes uncommitted on purpose. Home buttons, formatter churn, store metadata. Sometimes you need the pause—the space where you're not in the diff, not explaining yourself. Just sitting with what you made.
