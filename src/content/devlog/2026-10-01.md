Two separate systems went live today. They don't quite know about each other yet.

Morning was the road refactor. Spent weeks on that 0.70-cell radius approach—filled concentric outlines, supposed to look like Mini Motorways' atlas tiles. Except it didn't. Radii shrank on short legs, inside corners pinched, the whole geometry got strained. So this morning: ripped it all down to exact arc math. Quarter circles now. Radius 0.5 per cell. Built a new `roundedPolyline` function to handle the math—one stroked centreline per route, junctions included in the calculation, no separate pass. `Invader.locate` runs that arc math now instead of the old filled/concentric approach.

Removed everything that didn't fit: `cornerRadii`, `roadOutline`, `filletPolyline`, `truncatePolyline`, the whole `junctions` param. That deletion felt good—not because the code was bad, just because it was carrying weight we didn't need. Road width dropped from ~0.55 down to 0.45 cells. Cleaner. Faster. Committed at 12:23 (b3e5770, carried an unpushed 5a14d62) and then the picture broke immediately.

The cascades started right after. Enemy bodies—especially the super tank at 0.5 wide—started overhanging the narrower lanes. Two expensive paths forward now. Shrink all the bodies ~20% and the collision feel changes everywhere. Or widen roads back to 0.5 and the whole layout tightens, staircases compress, diagonals disappear. Haven't even seen the junction merge working on screen yet. The friction of an incomplete picture. Sometimes you need to see the break to understand the shape of the fix.

Evening was monetization. Phase 4 went live at 19:38 (a177ba6). Built the consent-aware post-game review flow with UMP readiness memoized—runs once, stays cached. Ads-impossible states keep retrying on their own schedule instead of stalling. Added the confirmed-no-fill schedule, a 60-second no-stack guard to prevent cascade loops. Wired the `onPurchased` callback to the shared `ReviewEngine`. The plumbing was invisible—works or breaks, no middle ground. Validation passed hard: 119 green, 2 skipped, analyzer clean.

One retroactive sting caught hours later: a daily survival test run posted to the real leaderboard. A loss at 0:43 that shouldn't have been there. Test data wasn't isolated. It was careless. Changed the test flow to campaign levels instead—isolation by execution context. The mistake is small, but the carelessness bothers more than the mistake itself. You weren't thinking carefully enough when you hit play.

Left some changes uncommitted on purpose. Inverted IconControl home buttons, formatter churn, store code still taking shape. Sometimes you need the pause—the space where you're not in the diff, not explaining yourself. Just sitting with what you made.
