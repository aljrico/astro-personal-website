Two separate systems went live today. They don't quite know about each other yet.

Morning was the road refactor. Spent weeks on that 0.70-cell radius approach—filled concentric outlines, supposed to look like Mini Motorways' atlas tiles. Except it didn't. Radii shrank on short legs, inside corners pinched, the whole geometry got strained. So this morning: ripped it all down to exact arc math. Quarter circles, radius 0.5 per cell. One stroked centreline per route using a new `roundedPolyline` function. Junctions included in the calculation, no separate pass.

Removed everything that didn't fit the new model: `cornerRadii`, `roadOutline`, `filletPolyline`, `truncatePolyline`, the whole `junctions` param. `Invader.locate` runs the arc math now, which is where it belonged all along. Cleaner. Faster. Made commit b3e5770 at 12:23 knowing the picture was broken.

Then the cascades started.

Road width dropped from ~0.55 down to 0.45 cells. Narrower roads meant enemy bodies—especially the super tank at 0.5 wide—started overhanging the lanes. Bodies weren't designed for this. The friction of an incomplete picture: I have two expensive paths forward now. Shrink all the bodies ~20% and the collision feel changes everywhere. Or widen roads back to 0.5 and the whole layout tightens, staircases compress, diagonals disappear. I haven't even seen the junction merge working on screen yet. Made the commit anyway. Sometimes you need to see the break to understand the shape of the fix.

Evening was monetization. Phase 4. Consent-aware post-game review flow with UMP readiness memoized while ads-impossible states keep retrying on schedule. Added the confirmed-no-fill schedule with a 60-second no-stack guard to prevent cascade loops. Wired the `onPurchased` callback to the shared `ReviewEngine`. The plumbing was invisible—works or breaks, no middle ground. Validation passed: 119 green, 2 skipped, analyzer clean. Made commit a177ba6 at 19:38.

One retroactive sting caught hours later: an idle daily survival run in the sim—lost at 0:43—posted to the real leaderboard under a test account. Test data wasn't isolated. It was careless. Changed the test flow to campaign levels instead. The mistake is small, but the carelessness bothers more than the mistake itself. You weren't thinking carefully enough when you hit play.

Left some changes uncommitted on purpose. Inverted home button icons, formatter churn, store config updates. Sometimes you need the pause—the space where you're not in the diff, not explaining yourself. Just sitting with what you made.
