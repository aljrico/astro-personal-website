Two separate systems went live today. They don't quite know about each other yet.

Morning was the road refactor (b3e5770). Exact arc math. Quarter circles at radius 0.5 per cell. One stroked centreline per route using a new `roundedPolyline` function. Pulled out the old cruft—`cornerRadii`, `roadOutline`, `filletPolyline`, `truncatePolyline`, the `junctions` param. Invader.locate runs exact arc math now, no approximations. Cleaner. Faster.

The previous attempt—0.70-cell radius with filled concentric outlines—pinched at inside corners. Worse than useless. Radii shrank on short legs, the whole thing looked strained. Mini Motorways uses atlas tiles; we're faking it with math but aiming at the same visual. This approach is more honest.

Then I saw what I'd missed. Road width went from ~0.55 down to 0.45 cells. Narrower means enemy bodies overhang the lanes now. Super tank is 0.5 wide. Doesn't fit. Options are expensive—shrink bodies ~20% and collision feel changes everywhere, or widen roads back to 0.5 and layouts tighten everywhere. Diagonal staircases are maybe for step 5, but I haven't seen the junction merge on screen yet. This is the cost of moving fast. Decisions compound before you see them all together.

I made the commit knowing the picture was incomplete.

Evening was monetization (a177ba6). Phase 4. Consent-aware post-game review flow. UMP readiness memoized while ads-impossible states keep retrying on schedule. Added the confirmed-no-fill schedule and a 60-second no-stack guard to prevent cascade loops. Wired the `onPurchased` callback to the shared `ReviewEngine`. The plumbing was invisible—works or breaks, no middle ground. Validation passed: 119 green, 2 skipped, clean analyzer. The whole thing lives or dies on its own.

One retroactive sting: an idle daily survival run posted to the real leaderboard. Test data wasn't isolated. A score from "NeonWhale9018" at 0:43 sitting there, proof I wasn't thinking carefully enough. Changed the test flow to campaign levels instead. The kind of mistake that stings because you know you should have caught it.

Left some changes uncommitted on purpose. Inverted icon controls, formatter churn, store metadata. Sometimes you need the pause—the space where you're not in the diff, not explaining yourself. Just sitting with what you made.
