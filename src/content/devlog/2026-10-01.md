Morning was ads. Phase 4 consent-aware review got locked in—the engine now knows when ads are impossible and stops thrashing, just quietly waits. Wired the monetization callback into the shared review stack. Tests ran clean. 119 passing. Two skipped. That's the kind of morning where you feel like you know what you're doing.

Then around midday I looked at the roads and realized they were wrong.

Not broken. Just... the 0.70-cell radius with concentric outlines I'd tried looked worse on screen than I'd imagined at the whiteboard. The corners pinched on short legs. Inside junctions felt cramped. So I ripped it out and switched to exact arc math—quarter circles, no compromise, one clean centerline per route. Simpler to reason about. Cleaner to render.

Width collapsed from 0.55 to 0.45 cells though, and now the enemy bodies overhang the lanes. They're 0.5 wide. Could shrink the tanks by 20%. Could go back to 0.5 road width. Could also punt this to the next phase and leave it as a known ugly. Haven't seen it on screen yet so maybe it's not as bad as it sounds.

Also: caught something uncomfortable. An idle daily survival run in the sim posted to the real leaderboard. 0:43 from a test account. It's a small thing—one garbage entry in what's still a sparse board—but that's the kind of thing that ruins credibility. Moving test runs to campaign levels. Not doing that again.

Diagonal staircases for step 5 are still a maybe. Everything else is in the working tree, uncommitted on purpose. The road math is tight. The ads flow is solid. But the day ends with questions, not answers, and tomorrow morning I'll have to pick a direction.

Some days you finish clean. Today I just stopped.
