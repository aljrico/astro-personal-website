Two separate systems went live today. They don't quite know about each other yet.

Morning was the road refactor. Exact arc math, quarter circles, 0.45-cell stroked centreline per route. The previous attempt—0.70-cell radius with filled concentric outlines—pinched at inside corners. Looked worse. Pulled the old code and committed. Mini Motorways uses atlas tiles; we're faking it with math.

Then I saw what I'd missed. Road width shrank from ~0.55 to 0.45 cells. Narrower means enemy bodies overhang. Super tank is 0.5 wide. Doesn't fit the lane. Options are expensive—shrink bodies ~20% and collision feel changes everywhere, or widen roads back to 0.5 and layouts tighten everywhere. Haven't seen the junction merge on screen yet. This is the cost of moving fast. Decisions compound before you see them all together.

I made the commit knowing the picture was incomplete.

Evening was monetization. Consent-aware post-game review flow. Wired `onPurchased` to the shared `ReviewEngine`. UMP readiness memoized. Ads-impossible states retry on schedule. 60-second no-stack guard. Validation passed—119 green, 2 skipped, clean analyzer. Invisible plumbing. Works or breaks. Today it worked.

One retroactive sting: an idle daily survival run in the sim lost and posted 0:43 to the real leaderboard. Test account data wasn't isolated. Caught it hours later. The kind of thing that stings because you weren't thinking carefully enough. Now testing on campaign levels instead.

Left some changes uncommitted on purpose. Home buttons, formatter churn, store metadata. Sometimes you need the pause—the space where you're not in the diff, not explaining yourself. Just sitting with what you made.
