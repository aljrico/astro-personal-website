Two separate systems went live today. They don't quite know about each other yet.

Morning was the road refactor (b3e5770, carrying an earlier unpushed commit from 5a14d62). Exact arc math. Quarter circles, radius 0.5 per cell. One stroked centreline per route using a new `roundedPolyline` function. Invader.locate now runs exact arc math instead of the old cruft—pulled out `cornerRadii`, `roadOutline`, `filletPolyline`, `truncatePolyline`, the `junctions` param. Cleaner. Faster. Matches what Mini Motorways does with atlas tiles, except we're faking it with math.

The previous attempt—0.70-cell radius with filled concentric outlines—pinched at inside corners. Radii shrank on short legs, the whole thing looked strained. Worse than useless. So I gutted it and went exact.

Then I saw the problem I'd missed. Road width went from ~0.55 down to 0.45 cells. Narrower means enemy bodies overhang the lanes now. Super tank is 0.5 wide. Doesn't fit. The open question: either shrink bodies ~20% and collision feel changes everywhere, or widen roads back to 0.5 and layouts tighten everywhere. Diagonal staircases are maybe for step 5, but I haven't seen the junction merge on screen yet. This is the cost of moving fast. Decisions compound before you see them all together.

I made the commit knowing the picture was incomplete.

Evening was monetization (a177ba6). Phase 4. Consent-aware post-game review flow. UMP readiness memoized while ads-impossible states keep retrying on schedule. 60-second no-stack guard to prevent cascade loops. Wired the `onPurchased` callback to the shared `ReviewEngine`. The plumbing was invisible—works or breaks, no middle ground. Validation passed: 119 green, 2 skipped, clean analyzer.

One retroactive sting: an idle daily survival run posted to the real leaderboard. Test data wasn't isolated. A score from someone at 0:43 sitting there—proof I wasn't thinking carefully enough when I hit play. Changed the test flow to campaign levels instead. The kind of mistake that stings because you know you should have caught it.

Left some changes uncommitted on purpose. Inverted icon controls on the home button, formatter churn, store metadata. Sometimes you need the pause—the space where you're not in the diff, not explaining yourself. Just sitting with what you made.
