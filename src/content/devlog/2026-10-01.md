Two separate systems went live today. They don't quite know about each other yet.

Morning was the road refactor (b3e5770). Exact arc math instead of the previous attempt—0.70-cell radius with filled concentric outlines. That approach pinched at inside corners and looked strained. Radii shrank on short legs. We're faking atlas tiles with math but aiming at the same visual. Mini Motorways gets 90/45 corners right; we needed the same.

Quarter circles now. Radius 0.5 per cell. One stroked centreline per route using a new `roundedPolyline` function. Invader.locate runs the exact arc math. Pulled out the old cruft: `cornerRadii`, `roadOutline`, `filletPolyline`, `truncatePolyline`, the `junctions` param. Cleaner. Faster.

But the math revealed what I'd missed. Road width shrank from ~0.55 down to 0.45 cells. Narrower means enemy bodies overhang the lanes now. Super tank is 0.5 wide. Doesn't fit anymore. The options are expensive either way—shrink bodies ~20% and collision feel changes everywhere, or go back to 0.5 width and everything tightens. Diagonal staircases are maybe step 5, but I haven't seen the junction merge on screen yet. Made the commit knowing the picture was incomplete.

Evening was monetization (a177ba6). Phase 4. Consent-aware post-game review flow. UMP readiness gets memoized while ads-impossible states keep retrying on schedule. Added the confirmed-no-fill schedule and a 60-second no-stack guard to prevent cascade loops. Wired the `onPurchased` callback to the shared `ReviewEngine`. The plumbing was invisible—works or breaks, no middle ground. Validation passed: 119 green, 2 skipped, clean analyzer.

One thing stung retroactively. An idle daily survival run in the sim lost (0:43, NeonWhale9018) and posted to the real leaderboard. Test data wasn't isolated. Caught it hours later. Changed the test flow to campaign levels instead. The kind of thing that stings because you weren't thinking carefully enough when you hit play.

Left some changes uncommitted on purpose. Inverted home button IconControl, formatter churn, store metadata. Sometimes you need the pause—the space where you're not in the diff, not explaining yourself. Just sitting with what you made.
