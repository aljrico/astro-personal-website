Two separate systems went live today. They don't quite know about each other yet.

Morning was the road refactor. Exact arc math. Quarter circles with a fixed 0.5-cell radius per route. One stroked centreline per route using a new `roundedPolyline` function, junctions included directly in the calculation. Pulled out the old cruft: `cornerRadii`, `roadOutline`, `filletPolyline`, `truncatePolyline`, the separate `junctions` param. All of it gone. `Invader.locate` runs the arc math in one pass now.

Why the pivot? The previous attempt—0.70-cell radius with filled concentric outlines—looked strained. Radii shrank on short legs. Inside corners pinched. Mini Motorways uses atlas tiles for their 90/45-degree corners; we're faking it with math but aiming at the same visual weight. The new approach is cleaner. Faster. Less strain. Committed b3e5770 at 12:23.

Then I saw what I'd missed. Road width dropped from ~0.55 down to 0.45 cells. Narrower means enemy bodies—super tanks are 0.5 wide—overhang the lanes now. The collision feel breaks everywhere. Options are both expensive: shrink all bodies ~20% and everything rebalances, or widen roads back to 0.5 and the whole layout tightens. Diagonal staircases might be a step 5 thing, but I haven't even seen the junction merge on screen yet to know if the arc math holds. Made the commit knowing the picture was incomplete. That friction stays with me.

Evening was monetization. Phase 4. Consent-aware post-game review flow wired properly. UMP readiness is memoized while ads-impossible states keep retrying on schedule. Added the confirmed-no-fill schedule and a 60-second no-stack guard to prevent cascade loops. Wired the `onPurchased` callback to the shared `ReviewEngine`. The plumbing was invisible—works or breaks, no middle ground. Validation passed: 119 green, 2 skipped, clean analyzer. Committed a177ba6 at 19:38.

One retroactive sting: an idle daily survival run in the test environment lost and somehow posted to the real leaderboard. Test data wasn't isolated. Caught it hours later. The kind of thing that stings because you weren't thinking carefully enough when you hit play. Changed the test flow to campaign levels instead. It's a small failure but it lodges. The carelessness bothers more than the mistake itself.

Left some changes uncommitted on purpose. Inverted IconControl home buttons, formatter churn, store/shipaton/. Sometimes you need the pause—the space where you're not in the diff, not explaining yourself. Just sitting with what you made.
