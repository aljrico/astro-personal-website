Two separate systems went live today. They don't quite know about each other yet.

Morning was the road refactor (b3e5770). Quarter circles, radius 0.5 per cell, exact arc math. One stroked centreline per route using a new `roundedPolyline` function. The previous attempt—0.70-cell radius with filled concentric outlines—looked worse than the thing it replaced. Radii shrank on short legs. Inside corners pinched. Strained. Mini Motorways pulls this off with atlas tiles; we're faking it with math but the goal is identical 90/45 corners.

Pulled out the old cruft: `cornerRadii`, `roadOutline`, `filletPolyline`, `truncatePolyline`, the `junctions` param. Invader.locate runs exact arc math now. Cleaner. Faster.

Then I saw the cost. Road width went from ~0.55 down to 0.45 cells to make the math work. Narrower means enemy bodies overhang the lanes. Super tank is 0.5 wide. Doesn't fit. Options are all expensive—shrink bodies ~20% everywhere and collision feel changes throughout, or widen roads back to 0.5 and layouts tighten everywhere. Diagonal staircases are maybe for step 5 anyway. Haven't seen the junction merge on screen yet. This is what happens when you move fast. Decisions compound before you see them all together.

I made the commit knowing the picture was incomplete.

Evening was monetization (a177ba6). Phase 4. Consent-aware post-game review flow. UMP readiness memoized while ads-impossible states keep retrying on schedule. 60-second no-stack guard to prevent cascade loops. Wired the `onPurchased` callback to the shared `ReviewEngine`. The plumbing was invisible—works or breaks, no middle ground. Validation passed: 119 green, 2 skipped, clean analyzer.

One retroactive sting: an idle daily survival run in the sim posted 0:43 to the real leaderboard under a test account. Test data wasn't isolated. Caught it hours later. The kind of thing that stings because you weren't thinking carefully enough when you hit play. Changed the test flow to campaign levels instead.

Left some changes uncommitted on purpose. Inverted icon controls on the home button. Formatter churn. Store metadata. Sometimes you need the pause—the space where you're not in the diff, not explaining yourself. Just sitting with what you made.
