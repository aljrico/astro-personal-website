Two separate systems went live today. They don't quite know about each other yet.

Morning was the road refactor. Exact arc math now—quarter circles, radius 0.5 per cell, one stroked centreline per route using a new `roundedPolyline` function. Yanked out the old approach: 0.70-cell radius with filled concentric outlines. That one pinched at inside corners, radii shrank on short legs, the whole thing looked strained. Mini Motorways uses atlas tiles; we're faking it with math but aiming at the same visual snap.

Pulled out years of cruft in the process: `cornerRadii`, `roadOutline`, `filletPolyline`, `truncatePolyline`, the `junctions` param. `Invader.locate` runs the arc math now. Junctions are included in the calculation—no separate pass. Cleaner. Faster. Committed b3e5770 at 12:23, carrying an unpushed commit from earlier.

But then the picture shifted. Road width came down from ~0.55 to 0.45 cells. Narrower means enemy bodies overhang the lanes. Super tank is 0.5 wide. Doesn't fit. Both fixes are expensive—shrink bodies ~20% and collision feel changes everywhere, or widen roads back to 0.5 and every layout tightens. Diagonal staircases maybe live at step 5, but I haven't seen the junction merge on screen yet. Made the commit knowing the picture was incomplete. That friction stays with me.

Evening was monetization. Phase 4. Consent-aware post-game review flow, wiring the ad precedence and UMP readiness. UMP successful states memoized while ads-impossible states keep retrying on schedule. Added the confirmed-no-fill schedule and a 60-second no-stack guard to prevent cascade loops. Then wired the `onPurchased` callback to the shared `ReviewEngine`. The plumbing was invisible—works or breaks, no middle ground. Validation passed: 119 green, 2 skipped, clean analyzer. Committed a177ba6 at 19:38.

One retroactive sting: an idle daily survival run in the sim—0:43—posted to the real leaderboard as test data. Caught it hours later. Changed the test flow to campaign levels instead. The kind of thing that stings because you weren't thinking carefully enough when you hit play. The carelessness bothers more than the mistake itself.

Left some changes uncommitted on purpose. Inverted home button IconControl, formatter churn, store metadata. Sometimes you need the pause—the space where you're not in the diff, not explaining yourself. Just sitting with what you made.
