Two separate systems went live today. They don't quite know about each other yet.

Morning was the road refactor. Spent weeks on that 0.70-cell radius approach—filled concentric outlines, supposed to look like Mini Motorways' atlas tiles. Except it didn't. Radii shrank on short legs, inside corners pinched, the geometry got strained. So this morning: ripped it all down to exact arc math. Quarter circles now. Radius 0.5 per cell. One stroked centreline per route using a new `roundedPolyline` function. Junctions included in the calculation, no separate pass. `Invader.locate` runs the arc math now instead of the old filled/concentric approach.

Removed everything that didn't fit the new model: `cornerRadii`, `roadOutline`, `filletPolyline`, `truncatePolyline`, the whole `junctions` param. Road width dropped from ~0.55 down to 0.45 cells. Cleaner. Faster. b3e5770 at 12:23—carried an unpushed commit with it (5a14d62)—and then the picture broke immediately.

The cascades started right after. Enemy bodies—especially the super tank at 0.5 wide—started overhanging the narrower lanes. Two expensive paths forward now. Shrink all the bodies ~20% and the collision feel changes everywhere. Or widen roads back to 0.5 and the whole layout tightens, staircases compress, diagonals disappear (that's a maybe for step 5 anyway). Haven't even seen the junction merge working on screen yet. The friction of an incomplete picture. Sometimes you need to see the break to understand the shape of the fix.

Evening was monetization. Phase 4 went live at 19:38. a177ba6. Rebuilt the post-game review flow around consent awareness and ad precedence. UMP readiness gets memoized while ads-impossible states keep retrying on schedule. Added the confirmed-no-fill schedule—a 60-second no-stack guard to prevent cascade loops. Wired the `onPurchased` callback to the shared `ReviewEngine`. Validation held: 119 green, 2 skipped, analyzer clean. The whole chain worked. The plumbing was invisible—works or breaks, no middle ground.

One retroactive sting caught hours later: an idle daily survival run—0:43, lost—posted to the real leaderboard under a test account. Test data wasn't isolated. It was careless. Changed the test flow to campaign levels instead. The mistake is small, but the carelessness bothers more than the mistake itself. You weren't thinking carefully enough when you hit play.

Left some changes uncommitted on purpose. Inverted IconControl home buttons, formatter churn, store-related pending code. Sometimes you need the pause—the space where you're not in the diff, not explaining yourself. Just sitting with what you made.
