Two separate systems went live today. They don't quite know about each other yet.

Morning was the road refactor. Exact arc math. Quarter circles, radius 0.5 per cell. One stroked centreline per route using a new `roundedPolyline` function. The previous attempt—0.70-cell radius with filled concentric outlines—pinched at inside corners, radii shrank on short legs, the whole thing looked strained. Mini Motorways uses atlas tiles; we're faking it with math but aiming at the same visual.

Ripped out the old cruft: `cornerRadii`, `roadOutline`, `filletPolyline`, `truncatePolyline`, the `junctions` param. All of it gone. `Invader.locate` runs exact arc math now, junctions included in the calculation. No separate pass. Cleaner. Faster. Committed b3e5770 at 12:23 (carried an unpushed 5a14d62).

Then the picture fractured. Road width dropped from ~0.55 down to 0.45 cells. Narrower means enemy bodies overhang the lanes now. Super tank is 0.5 wide—doesn't fit. Two paths forward, both expensive: shrink bodies ~20% and collision feel changes everywhere, or widen roads back to 0.5 and layouts tighten everywhere. Diagonal staircases are maybe for step 5, but I haven't seen the junction merge on screen yet. Made the commit knowing the picture was incomplete. That friction stays.

Evening was monetization. Phase 4. Consent-aware post-game review flow. UMP readiness is memoized while ads-impossible states keep retrying on schedule. Added the confirmed-no-fill schedule and a 60-second no-stack guard to prevent cascade loops. Wired the `onPurchased` callback to the shared `ReviewEngine`. The plumbing was invisible—works or breaks, no middle ground. Validation passed: 119 green, 2 skipped, clean analyzer. Committed a177ba6 at 19:38.

One retroactive sting: an idle daily survival run in the sim—0:43, lost—posted to the real leaderboard. Test data wasn't isolated. Caught it hours later. The kind of thing that stings because you weren't thinking carefully enough when you hit play. Changed the test flow to campaign levels instead. It's a small failure but it lodges. The carelessness bothers more than the mistake itself.

Left some changes uncommitted on purpose. Inverted IconControl home buttons, formatter churn, store/shipaton/. Sometimes you need the pause—the space where you're not in the diff, not explaining yourself. Just sitting with what you made.
