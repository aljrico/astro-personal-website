Two separate systems went live today. They don't quite know about each other yet.

Morning was the road refactor. b3e5770. Exact arc math. Quarter circles, radius 0.5 per cell. One stroked centreline per route using a new `roundedPolyline` function. `Invader.locate` runs the math clean now. Pulled out all the old cruft: `cornerRadii`, `roadOutline`, `filletPolyline`, `truncatePolyline`, the `junctions` param.

The previous attempt—0.70-cell radius with filled concentric outlines—looked worse. Radii shrank on short legs, inside corners pinched at the seams. Mini Motorways uses atlas tiles; we're faking it with math and aiming at the same visual. This approach gets there.

Cost: road width dropped from ~0.55 to 0.45 cells. Narrower means everything tightens. Enemy bodies don't fit anymore. Super tank is 0.5 wide—it overhangs the lanes now. Two expensive paths forward: either shrink all bodies ~20% and feel changes everywhere, or widen roads back to 0.5 and layouts compress. Haven't seen the junction merge on screen yet. This is what moving fast buys you—decisions compound before you see them all together.

I made the commit knowing the picture was incomplete.

Evening was monetization. a177ba6. Phase 4. Consent-aware post-game review flow. UMP readiness gets memoized while ads-impossible states keep retrying on schedule. Added a 60-second no-stack guard to prevent cascade loops. Wired the `onPurchased` callback to the shared `ReviewEngine`. The plumbing was invisible—works or breaks, no middle ground.

Today it worked. Validation ran 119 green, 2 skipped, clean analyzer.

Then the sting. An idle daily survival run in the sim lost and posted the score to the real leaderboard. Test data wasn't isolated. Caught it hours later. The kind of thing that stings because you weren't thinking carefully enough when you hit play. Changed the test flow to campaign levels instead. Now the boundary is explicit.

Left some changes uncommitted on purpose. Inverted icon controls on the home button, formatter churn, store metadata. Sometimes you need the pause—the space where you're not in the diff, not explaining yourself. Just sitting with what you made.
