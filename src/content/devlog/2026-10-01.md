Two separate systems went live today. They don't quite know about each other yet.

Morning was the road refactor. Exact arc math. Quarter circles, radius 0.5 per cell. One stroked centreline per route using a new `roundedPolyline` function. The previous attempt—0.70-cell radius with filled concentric outlines—pinched at inside corners. Worse than useless. Radii shrank on short legs, the whole thing looked strained. Mini Motorways uses atlas tiles; we're faking it with math but aiming at the same visual.

Pulled out the old cruft: `cornerRadii`, `roadOutline`, `filletPolyline`, `truncatePolyline`, the `junctions` param. Invader.locate runs exact arc math now. Cleaner. Faster. The junctions are included in the calculation. No separate pass. Pushed that (b3e5770) even knowing the picture was incomplete.

Here's what I missed: road width went from ~0.55 down to 0.45 cells. Narrower means enemy bodies overhang the lanes. Super tank is 0.5 wide. Doesn't fit. Options are expensive—shrink bodies ~20% and collision feel changes everywhere, or widen roads back to 0.5 and layouts tighten everywhere. Diagonal staircases are maybe for step 5, but I haven't seen the junction merge on screen yet. The collision question gets to stay open for now.

Evening was monetization. Phase 4. Consent-aware post-game review flow. UMP readiness memoized while ads-impossible states keep retrying on schedule. Added the confirmed-no-fill schedule and a 60-second no-stack guard to prevent cascade loops. Wired the `onPurchased` callback to the shared `ReviewEngine`. The plumbing was invisible—works or breaks, no middle ground. Validation passed: 119 green, 2 skipped, clean analyzer. That commit landed (a177ba6).

One retroactive sting: an idle daily survival run in the sim posted to the real leaderboard. Test data wasn't isolated. Caught it hours later, wired the test flow to campaign levels instead. The kind of thing that stings because you weren't thinking carefully enough when you hit play.

Left some changes uncommitted on purpose. Inverted home button IconControl, formatter churn, store metadata. Sometimes you need the pause—the space where you're not in the diff, not explaining yourself. Just sitting with what you made.
