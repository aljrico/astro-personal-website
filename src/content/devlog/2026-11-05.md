Gitignored `.asc/`. Local reports, nothing that belongs in the repo. Pushed it. Then sat there for a minute convinced I'd just moved v1.0.0 onto a nothing commit.

I hadn't. The tag still points at the build that actually went out. Tentative on the gitignore itself. Did it anyway. The reports can live on disk.

Wired RevenueCat into Play's real-time notifications. Topic for the game. Publisher access on that topic only.

I nearly added allUsers.

Hostia.

One checkbox. Looks like a convenience. I was already reaching for the next step. Something in the back of the head said look again. I looked.

That's the near miss. A private pipe that stayed private because the slow brain woke up in time. Gut wanted to ship. The other one got a veto. For once I let it.

Yesterday the iOS build went back in with the device video. Today I flipped it to auto-release. Play is in review too. Both stores. Waiting.

I am bad at this part. The interesting work is over. What's left is declaring trader status for the DSA and refreshing two consoles that will not move faster because I stared at them. A form. The least interesting door between here and live.

Finishing always does this. Restless. Eye already scanning for the next pasture while this one is still in someone else's queue.

So I went looking.

Pulled ASA attribution out of Verdoku and into flama. Flama-prefixed names so Verdoku's types don't collide when the pin upgrades. Same prefs keys, so the game can switch later without a migration story. Paused events skip the upload now instead of burning an attempt. Empty install id gets checked before opt-out, so a failed store read doesn't settle as forever.

SPM test showed Swift does not auto weak-link AdServices. No LC_LOAD_DYLIB. The explicit `-weak_framework` flag is required. The kind of thing you only find by checking, and then you feel slightly embarrassed you had to check.

Cookie got ad_paid from AdMob and the same ASA path through flama. Added it to the allowlist. Look at this. Look what I just moved. Two games sharing one attribution client that used to live inside one of them like it belonged there.

Then I locked all six consumers to that flama head. Lockfile-only. Cookie and conducir were sitting on stale pins for no real reason. The ones already on ref:main were silently one to three commits behind via their locks. Of course they were. That's how lockfiles work. You know it and you still forget until you look.

Wrote a bump script. Upgrade, analyze, commit the lock. Skip dirty trees. Skip docs-only. Felt like a system. Felt like I'd just closed a loop that would otherwise keep biting me.

Then I looked at it. Hardcoded project list. Every new game is another line. That's not a system. That's a pet.

Deleted it.

Pull model instead. ref:main everywhere. One rule: upgrade flama before you build. Zero maintenance. New games covered because they aren't on a list. Spreadsheet brain wanted the script. Gut said the script was the kind of knife-sharpening I always swear I don't do. This time I believed the gut.

Came out of the Apple Ads numbers. Campaign was roughly break-even. FR, DE, AU, US profitable. ES, GB, CA, NL not. I want measured per-campaign LTV before I put money back in. Spreadsheet brain, fully on. Could have declared the form and gone outside. Instead I ran the campaign math until the restlessness had somewhere to sit.

Still. Both stores. The game is in. I wanted to show someone.

Now we wait and see if this was the right week to stop tinkering.
