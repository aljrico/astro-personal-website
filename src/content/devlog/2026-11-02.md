Started with a bug that wasn't a bug.

Desert Hard 6. Someone reported it like the board was lying. I went in ready to fix logic. Then I sat with the screenshot and the board was fine. The player had read a barrel cactus as not-a-cactus, and "1 row south" as the same column. That's not a solver error. That's a language problem. The board was speaking cactus and the player was hearing something else.

So I did the Lanterntown thing. One cactus kind per Desert board. Layout revision 2. Campaign v2 gets the new recipe, v1 stays grandfathered, Daily cuts over on 2026-12-01. Added a shade guarantee while I was in there: non-saguaro boards get a spire, and every one-kind board keeps a saguaro or a spire shading an open cell. Goldens regenerated. Only desert moved. Legacy hashes byte-identical, which is the kind of quiet that makes me trust the rest of the day.

settings_sheet_test still has two failures. They were already on main. Not my circus today.

Then Cookie Empire pulled.

Redesigned the Mine expedition cards. I wanted them to feel like Verdoku's ticking stopwatch: odds on every reward, a separate red death chance, a small flat send button. Named every depth. The Shallows down to The Last Light. Each one gets a single line. That's it. One line has to carry the whole mood of going deeper.

Ran a writing bake-off. Five models. Grok, Codex, Fable, Sonnet, Opus. All of them came out too Hemingway. Sparse in the way that thinks it's profound. I rewrote four lines myself and used those as the style reference. Look at this. The titles and descriptions blur into focus at random times, only when they scroll into view. Locked tiers stay blurred. It looks like a game now. Not a spreadsheet wearing a costume.

Gave the cards more air inside. Two bumps, by eye.

Deleted the screenshot tests. The golden ones. For good. They changed one decision in weeks and cost minutes on every iteration. New rule: change, hot reload, analyze, I look at it myself. Code quality is overrated. If I can see it, I don't need a robot to photograph it for me.

Late afternoon I retired the per-repo engineering diary. Commits and memories already cover it. I was writing the same day twice and calling it process. Then I found out the scratch reminder for this devlog had never worked. Wrong event check. User-only message. The thing that was supposed to catch the day had been silently failing the whole time. Fixed it. Committed the retirement. The hook fired on that commit. First time it has ever reached the model. I killed one diary and the other one woke up for the funeral.

The diary now drafts with Grok 4.6 and falls back to Haiku. Grok's CLI kept acting like a coding agent, going looking for files that aren't there, until its Claude-compat discovery and tools got switched off. The machine wanted to be an engineer. I needed a writer. Sometimes that's the whole job: telling the tool which room it's in.

There's a cost to this. Agentic speed is heroin. Direct, don't type, keep moving. But the idle thoughts used to arrive mid-keystroke. Today I noticed them arriving after. Or not arriving. Hard to tell which.

Went back to the Expeditions tab. The Crystaller cards got the same treatment as the mine: same padding, a much bigger ore cost, more room for the description. Pulled the Craft button into the same flat pill as Send. One widget. I was going to copy it. I didn't.

Then every room got its own light. Slate grey forge for the Smith. Tower violet for the Crystaller. A bonfire coming up from the bottom of the Hollows. The Fall kept its Hell gradient. Moved the light off-centre so the tabs stop sitting like a matching set. Switches glide now.

At first the glide felt late. I was ready to chase a delay. It was the ease-in-out curve dragging its feet. Switched to ease-out and it snaps. Hostia. Look at this. I kept flipping Smith, Crystaller, Hollows, just to watch the room change temperature. This is the stuff.

Then Verdoku again. A simplify pass over everything since v1.21.0. Four reviewers, two agents, me. Sixteen files. Nothing behaves different. I keep saying code quality is overrated and then I let a cleanup eat the hour. It felt productive. Was it?

Skipped the deeper stuff. Islands, hero art, ad slots, recipe ids, gating. Then I spawned agents for most of it anyway. The next hour was me pointing and them typing. That cost from earlier, in practice.

The home hero used to flash the 1.6MB control art on cold start, even when the experiment had already picked another arm. First launch goes blank for a second and a half now, same for every arm. The control art stays out of the first frame. Last settled arm lives in prefs and gets read before the app comes up. Settled assignment still wins. I skipped a precache. The first frame already asks for the right asset.

Boards now remember which recipe made them. Old ones infer: seed, Desert v2's namespace, Endless and Daily as revision 1. Stale Endless and Daily caches regenerate unless you already started or solved them. This morning's cactus work was layout revision 2. This evening it's generation revision, and Islands campaign v2 is revision 3. I was still carrying December 1 in my head. Same calendar. The language problem, one biome over.

The Islands tuning was never reaching Endless or Daily. Easy and Medium supposed to misread more. Hard supposed to cap what sits on the map. I measured it once it landed. Tuning alone barely moves the strong-misreading gate. Easy 7 to 17 percent. Hard 2 to 10. The shore rule is doing the real work. The hard cap costs yield: 12 percent below floor with no budget, 33 at 500ms. Twelve golden hashes moved. Shipped it anyway. I could have lived in those percentages for the rest of the day. I didn't.

Then I dropped the cutover. Latest revision ships on release day. Mixed leaderboards, fine. The December date lasted from morning until the merge. Gut call. The shore rule is still sitting there untouched. I can feel that one waiting.

One agent hit a Riverpod 2 trap. Unlistened FutureProvider, invalidated mid-await, never rebuilds, .future hangs forever. Sat with it long enough to feel stupid. Then it was a missing listener. Held a subscription so it can't forget itself. Skipped the drip-timer. Timing risk. Gut call.

Wanted a seed shortcut for the migration. Didn't take it. Another agent was rewriting how recipes get identified, and copying the rule would have silently diverged. Decode the case, ask the campaign. Pixel-diffed a timer badge in a throwaway test because a saveLayer was shifting glyphs by a third of a pixel. Unbounded layer, it settles.

Solver is ten to fifteen percent faster. Same answers, same order, 78 boards. Dropped a used-cell set I didn't need. Read that and thought I used to be sharper. Then I remembered I am the person who left the extra set in.

Quiet plumbing, then a deletion. Analytics can pause events now, host-persisted, so Verdoku keeps its own flag and enrollment sits pending until it's allowed to speak. Then I ripped out the old opt-out wrappers. 185 lines of prefs sniffing that had been eating enrollments while you were opted out. They send after opt-in now. The SDK holds the pause. Verdoku just asks. Killing that felt better than writing it.

settings_sheet_test still has two failures. They survived a cactus, a simplify pass, and a swarm. Still not my circus.

Cookie Empire pulled again. Hell's souls became blood. One drop per dead grandma, fed to the Yokai on a 10 to 1000 ladder. Flirted with reworking every effect. Kept them. Blood is never saved. Derived from lifetime losses minus what the feedings cost. No migration, no sync drift. Simplest idea of the day.

A lost crew's blood flies to its counter when the journal closes. No words. Understated, brutal. I kept closing the journal just to watch the drop go. This is the stuff.

Found every flying-currency animation had been landing about ten points off since the port. Now they land on the real icon. You stop seeing a miss like that until something you care about misses.

Dropped the ascend-first gate on the root Yokai while I was in there. Blood flows from the first lost crew. No reason to make anyone sit on a hoard staring at a locked tree.

Called it: the Swift app is dead. Flutter only from here. I can feel the door close. I'll tell myself I might open it again. I probably won't.

Mac clipboard died in the middle of this. Hung pasteboard daemon. Every copy gone. Killed the process, it came back. Joder. The trivial ones always sting more.

Redesigned the permanent boost card in the gem store. Outlined title, pale-mint PERMANENT, a live cookies-per-second payoff pill. Gold was wrong. A full-width band was wrong. Then be dry: extract the price pill, share it with the skin card. The gate caught a live blur I wasn't supposed to ship, and a line-count ratchet. The blur was real. The line count was a ritual. Dropped the glow, raised the budget, moved on.

I already knew the sheet you open from the home counter was next. Terrible UI. I said it out loud.

And then the circus was mine. settings_sheet_test was never an app bug. The sheet grew. Rows below 420px fold. Teardown leak. Fixed it. Full suite green. 1377. Two failures through a cactus and a swarm, and they were a fold and a leak. Of course they were.

Started a 1.22.0 test release. The ship script died instantly. Still calling the Lion boundary script we already deleted. Nobody had built since Lion came out. Removed the call. Venga. New SDK RPCs are live in prod. I checked.

Registered the new experiment rows in prod, disabled. Hero art and free progression. Enable after this build is live. The free progression row didn't even exist yet. That's how far ahead of the actual ship I was this morning.

1.22.0 (67) is on TestFlight and Play internal. VALID. I want a phone in my hand before prod. Note drafted, approved, sitting there. Skipped a temp-index reset once and left a phantom staged delete. Harmless. Still felt sloppy.

Rebuilt the permanent boost sheet from first principles. It has to sell. Wing on the item, x2 on the rays, chips for never expires and kept when you ascend, a white payoff card, a big CTA. Checked that ascend never wipes purchases before I wrote that sentence. v2 was a purple box in a white sheet, built blind. Even worse. v3 I screenshot. Faked a three-boost state. Looked at it myself, the way the new rule says. Then a shine pass. The value shines, the button a beat later, every two seconds.

Perfect.

For about a minute. Then the eye is already somewhere else.

Pulled the environment name off the Daily leaderboard. I didn't want it there. Missed build 67. It rides the next one, if prod happens.

Prod is waiting on me looking at a phone.

The unfinished stuff is still unfinished. Ad slots, gating, a drip-timer I didn't trust, a shore rule I measured and left alone. I'll come back. I always say that. Nothing is abandoned.

Was the optimism justified. The cards snapped. The cactus got simpler. The diary is awake. Hell looks like Hell. Agents shipped the things I wasn't going to type. The Swift door closed. 1.22.0 is on TestFlight and I'm still holding prod. Today, yes.
