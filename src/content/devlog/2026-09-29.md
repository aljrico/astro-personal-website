Morning was the road system. Ditched the concentric circles approach — the radius was shrinking on short segments and the inner corners pinched. Too fussy. Replaced it with a simple quarter-circle per cell, 0.5 radius, one stroked centerline. Removed cornerRadii, roadOutline, filletPolyline, truncatePolyline. The whole parameterization fell away once I stopped trying to be clever.

It looked better. Immediately. That's the thing about visual polish — you know it when you see it. The decision took seconds. The code cleanup was just following the visual decision downhill.

Pushed it at lunch. Felt good.

Then evening came. Started running the ads flow through validation. Monetization wired to ReviewEngine. Tests passing — 119 of them. The consent-aware review state works. The UMP readiness memoization is solid. No-fill schedule and the 60-second guard are in place.

But then I ran an idle daily survival sim to spot-check and it posted 0:43 to the real leaderboard under NeonWhale9018.

Not great.

The test infrastructure is tangled enough that I wasn't testing on campaign levels, so a random run that should've been harmless leaked through. Found it because I noticed the leaderboard timestamp, not because the tests caught it. That's the kind of thing that makes you feel competent and useless at the same time.

Also: the road width is now 0.45 cells. The super tank is 0.5 wide. So there's a gap where the bodies overhang the road. Either shrink the bodies twenty percent or go back to 0.5 width. Haven't seen it on screen yet to know if it's catastrophic or just janky.

Didn't commit the home button inversion or the formatter stuff. Left it loose.

The road looks right. The ads logic is tested. The infrastructure still has gaps. Some days you get two wins and three new problems, and you have to decide if that's progress.
