Morning was roads. Spent weeks convinced the filled concentric outlines with radius-0.7 were the right answer — proper energy, mathematically sound. Pushed it early afternoon and the payoff landed: exact arc math, quarter circles, one stroked centreline per route via the new roundedPolyline. Invader.locate uses exact arc math now. Deleted cornerRadii, roadOutline, filletPolyline, truncatePolyline — all those helper functions that felt necessary when I wrote them. Shipped as b3e5770.

The roads look better. Simpler, less cluttered. That moment when deleting code also deletes visual noise. Rare and good.

Then the downside showed up immediately. Road width went from 0.55 to 0.45 cells. Enemy bodies and lane drift now overhang the pavement. The super tank is 0.5 wide — you can see it spilling onto the grass. Either shrink the bodies by 20% or walk this back. Haven't decided yet. There's a version that works. Just not sure it's this one.

Caught something in the leaderboard data that afternoon. An idle daily survival run somehow got posted to the real leaderboard. NeonWhale9018, 0:43 — real entry, real problem. The kind of anomaly that jumps out when someone actually looks at the stats. Redirected test runs to campaign levels instead. Small fix, large weight.

Evening was monetization. Phase 4: consent-aware post-game review, ad precedence, UMP readiness memoized, confirmed-no-fill schedule, 60-second no-stack guard. Wired the onPurchased callback to the shared ReviewEngine. Shipped as a177ba6. 119 tests passed, 2 skipped, analyzer clean. It's invisible infrastructure work — the kind nobody wants to watch happen. But it has to happen.

Left some uncommitted on purpose: icon tweaks, formatter noise, sketch code. The roads are today's story.

The narrower roads are a problem I didn't anticipate. Probably shrink the tanks tomorrow. But the refactor itself feels right — cleaner code, cleaner visuals, cleaner rules. Sometimes you need the visual payoff to forgive the complications it creates.
