Morning was roads. The 0.70-cell radius filled outlines were pinching at inside corners, shrinking on short legs — it looked worse the more I looked at it. Ripped it all out. Swapped to exact arc math with fixed quarter-circle geometry and a single stroked centerline. Cleaner. Less code. Looks better on screen.

The cost: roads are narrower now. 0.45 cells instead of 0.55. Which means enemy bodies and lane drift now overhang the pavement. Super tank is 0.5 wide, so it's sitting half on, half off. Two paths: shrink the bodies twenty percent or backpedal to 0.5 cell roads. Neither is great. One's too much art, one's giving up the visual. Haven't decided yet. Leaving it for tomorrow's head.

Removed cornerRadii, roadOutline, filletPolyline, truncatePolyline. The geometry simplification freed up maybe fifty lines. Invader.locate was already using exact arcs, so the refactor just made everything consistent. One way to measure it: junctions should look right now. Haven't seen them on screen yet.

Caught something: an idle daily survival run somehow posted a loss to the real leaderboard. 0:43. NeonWhale9018. Test on campaign levels from now on, not survival. Good catch before that becomes a pattern.

Evening: monetization. Phase 4 consent-aware post-game review and ad precedence. Wired the purchase event to the shared ReviewEngine. Validation passed — 119 tests, 2 skipped, analyzer clean. The memoization is working; UMP successful readiness stays cached while impossible states keep retrying. The sixty-second no-stack guard is in. It's done.

Left a few things uncommitted on purpose. Inverted some home buttons but the change felt shallow, didn't push it. Formatter churn in store/shipaton/ — can clean that later. Sometimes you know a commit is going to be noise before you make it.

Two moves landed. One problem opened. That's the day.
