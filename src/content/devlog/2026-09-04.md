Morning was the purchase sheet. Built it clean from the mockup — header, cards transitioning through their lifecycle, gem button anchored at the bottom. Forked the gem upgrade sheet and cut everything that didn't belong. 

The palette work felt right. The colors landed without fighting. Spacing constants, ring widths dialed in. That moment when the visual clicks into place before you even ship it — when the bounce and the saturation and the negative space all say *yes, this is it*. That's the thing. That's why I code.

Pushed it, then the context switch. Release branch needed notifications. Twenty localization files threaded through with new keys. Necessary work. The kind that ships invisibly because nobody celebrates correct strings — they only notice when they're wrong.

Afternoon, back to 6.0. Restored a deleted component, rewired the tap handler. Small surgery. But the real win was quieter and bigger.

Built a Fast scheme. App-target-only build, skips the simulator reinstall tax on every cycle. Warm iterations down to 4-5 seconds. On a single build, who cares. On the tenth build, the twentieth — it compounds into flow-state that doesn't get interrupted by waiting. Patched the global build-ios skill too, so this applies everywhere now.

Infrastructure work is invisible until you're not grinding your teeth. Then you notice it quietly, in the space it creates.
