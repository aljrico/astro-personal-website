The day split three ways.

Morning was flow. Rebuilt the permanent-boost purchase sheet from the mockup — header, card stack, gem button, all the visual spacing locked down. Those moments when the screen becomes what you pictured. Not flashy, just *right*. Purples for the gems, deep greens for the buttons, one constant for the ring widths. The kind of detail that matters because it's invisible when it's done.

Then context-switched to release work. New-case notification copy, localizations across a bunch of files. Necessary. Not exciting. The dutiful kind of work that has to happen but doesn't pull you in.

Late morning grabbed back the main-line work. Restored a deleted view, rewired the sheet interaction through a closure. Small fixes. But then — Fast scheme. Build configuration that drops warm compiles from 90 seconds to 4 or 5. That's the kind of win that pays dividends. Every iteration loop faster. The friction just evaporates.

So three separate pieces across three branches, but each one solved something. Polish. Maintenance. Developer experience. Not a particularly elegant day, but it moved. Some days that's enough.
