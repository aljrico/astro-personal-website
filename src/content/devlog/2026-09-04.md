Then at 13:31 the afternoon shifted again. Back to 6.0. Two things landed.

Then the real moment: the Fast scheme. This isn't elegant architecture. It's not a refactoring. It's an app-target-only build configuration that lives outside the normal scheme entirely—normal scheme stays untouched. Simulator reinstall migrates the data container GUID and re-resolves paths. Warm builds went from ninety seconds to four or five because the reinstall tax vanishes. Frictionless iteration. The global build-ios skill got patched (outside the repo, makes sense for this one). Icon swap—dreamcatcher-rhos—pending but the build performance landed. This is the improvement that compounds so quietly you don't notice it until six months later when you realize you've shipped twice as much because the feedback loop was shorter. Committed 3544167b, pushed.

Three branches. Three pushes. One day. The momentum is real.

Unfinished weight in the background: the environment case notification scheduler still needs its trigger logic, docs/notifications.md is written but unstaged, CONTRACT_REVIEW and marketing-captures are untracked. This is the rhythm—ship, see the next twenty things, decide what matters. The work doesn't end. It reshuffles.

Morning will say what's next.
