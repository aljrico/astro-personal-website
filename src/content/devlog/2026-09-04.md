Morning started with the purchase sheet. Rebuilt it from the trimmed mockup—header, CURRENT/NEXT BOOST cards, gem button forked off the existing upgrade sheet. The code moved clean. But the palette work was where the session lived. Purples threading from gem to wing family. Greens cascading from caribbean into the button colors—the whole thing cohesive. Ring widths all locked to one constant. Stack pill through x32. These aren't decisions that move the funnel. When they're wrong you feel it in your bones. When they're right—three passes to get there—you *see* it. Committed 55510540, pushed to 6.0.

Mid-day shifted branches. Notifications work on release/1.17.0. Copy and localizations. Twenty files in l10n, boilerplate that's necessary and glamorous only in retrospect. New case notification keys: newCaseAvailableNotificationTitle, environmentCaseReadyNotificationBody. The scheduler code stayed unstaged—the actual trigger logic, the waking mechanism—but the surface layer shipped. That's the tradeoff today: the messages are out. The delivery mechanism waits. Committed 4d89d8f, pushed.

Then the afternoon shifted again. Back to 6.0. Restored the permanent-boost wing badge—deleted it earlier and the tap flow needed rewiring through the sheet closure. Clean. But the real moment was the Fast scheme.

This isn't elegant architecture. It's not a refactoring. It's an app-target-only build configuration that bypasses everything the normal scheme touches. Warm builds went from ninety seconds to four or five. The simulator doesn't reinstall the container, doesn't re-resolve paths. Iteration gets *frictionless*. This is the improvement that compounds so quietly you don't notice it until six months later when you realize you've shipped twice as much because the feedback loop was shorter. Patched the global build-ios skill, committed both pieces (82fa03f1, 3544167b), pushed.

Three branches. Three pushes. One day. The momentum is real.

Unfinished weight in the background: the environment case notification scheduler still needs its trigger logic, the icon swap is pending, the contracts and marketing captures are sitting untracked. This is the rhythm—ship, see the next twenty things, decide what matters. The work doesn't end. It reshuffles.

Morning will say what's next.
