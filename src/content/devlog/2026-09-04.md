Three out the door. Purchase sheet redesign first—rebuilt the whole thing. Header, boost cards, gem button. The palette work was the part that held me. Purples for gems, caribbean melting into the button family. Ring widths all the same constant. These details don't move the needle for anyone else, but when they're right, they're *right*. You can feel it.

Notifications after. Copy, localizations. The unglamorous stuff. Necessary. Shipped.

Then the Fast scheme. Warm builds went from ninety seconds to four or five. That's the move. Not technically elegant—just app-target-only, normal scheme untouched. But it changes *how you work*. Iteration gets faster, feedback loops shrink, testing becomes frictionless. This is the compound interest kind of improvement. The kind you don't notice day-to-day but notice years later.

Three commits, all out. Momentum feels real.

Background hum of unfinished: notification scheduler still hanging, icon swap pending, contracts and marketing assets sitting untracked. This is the part where you ship something and immediately see the twenty other things that need attention. Normal. The work never ends.

Morning will decide what's next.
