Morning was the leak bug. Lives were dropping early because the HUD held back one life per flight, but shells cost three and tanks cost five. Most of the damage got eaten before the counter caught up. Now the number that drops is the number actually taken. Satisfying in the dull way.

Then a cleanup pass. Chills are a plain list plus a per-tower map, so Frost's aiming is cheap again. I checked that the search results didn't change with Frost in play, which is the only kind of test I trust. I also found two things I'm not fixing today. Frost's "avoid recently chilled" logic fights stacking, and the kill pay on screen (5, 10, 15) isn't what you actually get. The UI is lying a little. Noted. Tomorrow's problem.

Someone changed the leak costs under me. Glider is now 1, Great Old Glider is 3. The balance screens still show the old numbers, so those need a recheck. I'd have liked the numbers to hold still for one day. They didn't.

A collaborator's backdrop blur behind the tower picker landed too. I reviewed it, approved it, then reworked it to share one backdrop read, because it would have hit the GPU once per box. It looks right. That kind of polish makes me happy for about four seconds.

Build 35 went up to TestFlight before lunch, internal group added. Export broke. There are two distribution certs in the keychain, one expiring in a couple of days, and Xcode grabbed the wrong one. I got through it with a manual profile. Joder, that cost an hour I didn't plan.

The afternoon was the good part. Super waves, the endless ones past wave 10 that get tougher every 30 seconds, were basically invisible. A 9px moon turning yellow. So I built a night. A dark wash over the whole board, a moon-blue multiply with a vignette that deepens each night. A banner that types "Night falls" the first time and slams "Night n" in after that. A heavy haptic, a thump, and falling glass chimes on the first one. At night the wave bar collapses into one countdown. I ran it in the simulator and kept triggering it just to watch. That's the feeling: the screen doing exactly what I pictured in my head.

I also added a debug-only long press to skip straight to night, so I can test it without playing ten waves first. Skipped runs don't post scores. Good.

Build 36 went up after that. Processed, valid, into the internal group. This time the manual export just worked, which is the best thing you can say about signing.

I shipped this into basically nothing. Internal testers only. Nobody's going to notice build 36 except me, and that's fine, it's a build, not a launch. But I already caught myself scanning for what's next. Finished things feel like a chore to sit with.
