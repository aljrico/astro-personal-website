The day had two tempos. Morning was all detail work — the stuff that feels productive because you're constantly moving: swapping colors on region cards, bundling a new font, repositioning elements until they breathe right. The muddy palette came alive with pastels. Campaign cards got a Play tag that shouldn't work but does. Heros font everywhere except the logo, which stayed ARCO, which probably matters less than I think.

Then the home page. Dropped the wordmark entirely. City grid instead. Wrote a painter for it — static streets, faint grid, the whole thing animated with invaders walking routes like they actually live there. The landing animation staggered in, all that easeOutBack polish. It worked immediately. No debate. That's rare.

Shipped TestFlight 1.1.0 at 18:45 — new roster, six-dart colonies instead of five motes, the whole invader family settled. Built from clean checkout so the parallel work stayed out. Everything landed.

The momentum carried into the evening. Started exploring a sandbox for a new map idea. Canal. Not the full thing — just a sketch in a worktree, nothing pushed. But the iteration was *stupid* fast. Black sea at the top, white lake at the bottom, canal in between with critters flowing down. Death animations mirrored (eye flies to a counter), leak animations in red, tower reach shown as a hairline ring with the water inside lit white. Tower drag-to-build with the ghost snapping to grid. A reagent counter that counts up one-by-one so you feel the bounty.

By the end I'd redrawn the entire HUD three times. Swapped the tower picker from boxes to morphing panels. Added a warning line where critters start blinking. Cut things, added things, cut them again. The kind of evening where you look up and four hours are gone but everything on screen feels inevitable, like it was always supposed to be this way.

No deployment blocker stress. No "should I be doing this instead." Just the work talking to itself and the decisions coming fast enough that the overthinking never lands.

One Verdoku security commit earlier — timer-freeze exploit hardened, server-side validation ready. Worth noting but not today's heartbeat.

The unfinished things sit quiet. They'll be there tomorrow.
