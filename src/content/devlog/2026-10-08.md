Started the day fast. Campaign cards got new region colors—coral, sky blue, periwinkle. Bundled TeX Gyre Heros to replace Helvetica so it works on Android. The lens view now shows the coast instead of nothing. Pushed. TestFlight 1.1.0+15.

Spent hours redesigning invaders. Four concepts narrowed down to one roster: tadpole motes that kick and glide, water flea swarms that hop and drift, mantas, six-dart colonies. The game changed with it—colonies now burst into six real invaders instead of five motes. Updated the help text. Pushed again.

By afternoon the campaign had moved onto the home map itself. One big city grid with the Field Guide as a second district, region levels below on a half-grid lattice. Camera pans over empty streets, cards drop in staggered with easeOutBack. Tested it via timeDilation and screenshots. The motion reads right.

Switched to Verdoku midway—timer exploitation, server sync issues, database state drifted. The migration's written but blocked. Different kind of work. Investigation, hardening. It'll sit.

The real depth happened in a worktree, uncommitted. Canvas redesign. An entirely different game mode.

Started with four browser concepts. Settled on one: canal on grey grid, black sea at the top, white lake at the bottom. Critters spawn from an Old Well. Tap a cell, place tower, drag to build. The objective is pure: fill the canal, leak a critter to the houses, lose a life. Reagent currency, shoot to earn, upgrade or sell.

HUD got stripped down—wave bar on top, one line below: gear, homes, reagent, time. No level name, no bloat. Currency counter ticks up on kills, digits swap fast so it reads as rolling. Bounty impacts flash yellow. Leaks collapse the critter into its eye, then the eye flies red to the homes counter.

Tower picker redesigned three times. Settled on: name on the edge, square rank pips, UPGRADE button (two slots wide with next rank peeking), SELL. Reach preview shows coverage in tower color with the water lit white inside. Drag ghost floats above the thumb, snaps to grid, watermelon when invalid.

Got stuck in AnimatedSwitcher weirdness—rapid count-down left digits ghosted and sitting low. Fixed by swapping with Duration.zero and everything settled.

Then the details. Warning line is dashed watermelon red, one row from the lake. Critters that cross it blink red at 4Hz but stay targetable. Death juice for shot critters: body bursts into seven spinning chunks and white flash, five shards in tower color. Leak juice: eye flies red. Splash foam tints with the water it lands on. Shore lowered for more map.

Every iteration was collaborative—try this, user says no, keep that, drop the other thing. Wordmark became Chakra Petch Bold. Invaders locked after hours of rejections. The Canvas started as four concepts and became one unified thing.

Nothing's shipped. It's all worktree, experimental, uncommitted. But it moves right. Colors read. The objective is clear.

One thing flagged: the new home page aesthetic is Mini Motorways-adjacent. Same city grid, same feel, walking invaders at game pace. Wordmark "Mini TD." in Chakra Petch Bold. It looks great. But it looks *like* them. Trademark risk is real. That's tomorrow's problem to solve.

Also: performance agent measured the new swimmer art at +65% CPU, +16% GPU draw time on simulator. Possible solutions—cut outline samples, precompute half-width tables. Haven't measured on device yet.
