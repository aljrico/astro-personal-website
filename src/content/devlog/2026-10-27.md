Venga, bumped: analytics went to 0.2.0 and the leaderboard package to 0.5.0. Analyze came back clean and 146 tests passed. Until someone applies it, every debug build's events get rejected. Release is unaffected. It's the kind of gap that's invisible until you go to test it, and I'd rather know about it now than find it at 2am.
