Play rejected Tentatively 1.0.0 (10) at 08:49. Reason: "keeps stopping." On launch. The sentence you never want from a store you already submitted to.

Reproduced it on the emulator with the exact AAB I had shipped. So the store was right. I had shipped a brick.

R8. AGP 9. It stripped WorkDatabase_Impl's constructor because AdMob pulls WorkManager in, and then it renamed a Kotlin Continuation inside the shared_preferences JNI signatures, so prefs died quietly on top of the crash. Two corpses. One minify pass.

Joder.

Keep rules in proguard-rules.pro. Build 11. Fresh install, solve, kill the app, open it again, progress still there. The boring kind of win. The one you only celebrate because the alternative was a listing that launches into a grave.

The lesson is embarrassing in the way obvious lessons are. Smoke-test the release AAB on Android before you press submit. Debug had been lying to me the whole time. I could have known yesterday. I found out from Play.

Side quest while I was in there: brew's bundletool dragged Java 27 onto the machine. Gradle 9.1 took one look and sat down. Hostia. Pointed JAVA_HOME at 17, built, moved on. Not every fight gets a thesis.

Evening was a different app. Different temperature.

Shipped 6.0 (51) to Play internal and the TestFlight internal groups. Play 503'd once. Retried. It went through. That was the whole ceremony. No parade. A number moved and I wanted to show someone anyway.

Then I started deleting.

First pass was the civil one. A PendingPurchase mixin. A landing currency indicator. Gem sheets and card frames sharing a face instead of each carrying their own. 127 lines gone. I left the garden layout alone. Left the shell. Left blur and scenery snapshotting. Cleanup night is a bad night to rewire the room. The expected value on "while I'm here" is a prettier crash, and I had already done that once today.

Second pass got meaner. The dead Hell refund card. Sheet faces nobody was looking at. About 400 lines. Returns already shrinking. The reviewers coming back with leftover dupes instead of whole organs. That's how you know you're at the bottom of the barrel, or close enough that the barrel is a choice.

I flagged two things and did not touch them. Garden tooltip arrow sits wrong on the edge columns. Kraken shadow shows up after you lose one grandma. Both real. Both someone-else's-problem, except the someone is me in a future tense I have a poor track record with. They'll wait. They always wait.

Then the agent went through the tree and found 825 more lines that could just leave. I read all seven diffs. Applied all seven. Directing, not typing. The good drug. The biggest one was an unmounted gem scythe still dragging its entire sim around behind it. A whole machine for a thing that was not even on screen. Past me built a scythe. Present me threw the scythe in a skip. I don't know which one was the genius.

Garden fixture traces now stop at the first skip step. The skip API is gone. Swift is dead, so I don't care. If it launches, it launches.

Pushed main. Three commits. Fourteen hundred and fifty lines that do not exist anymore.

Look at that. Look how much smaller it got.

The high is already thinning. What's left is a garden I refused to rework, a tooltip I postponed, and a morning where the other app arrived at Play as a crash. I woke up thinking Tentatively was out. It wasn't. I went to bed with a lighter tree and a keep-rules file I should have written last week. Both of those are the job. Tonight's version of enough is: the AAB opens, the internals have a build, and a scythe that wasn't doing anything finally isn't.
