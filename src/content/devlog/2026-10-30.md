Morning was Survival's day and night split. Gliders wait for dark now. The first Great Old Glider and its colony show up on wave 10, and after that nights are about three quarters gliders. Days belong to wigglers. Whatever health the gliders were holding back gets handed to the wigglers, and skipper packs pay out the coins that were held back. On paper it feels right. The game gets to vote later.

Tested Ember at 300 and rejected it. Ember went to 13/16 levels, Bloom dropped to 12/16, and the spend just slid over to Needle. Nope.

Bloom's day lean changed too. Tough darts became tough wigglers, because darts were building an ~80-dart flood at the finale. Colonies count as bosses now. I'd have argued against that at breakfast and defended it by lunch. Fine.

Then the real run: 16 dailies at beam 40. Ember hit 16/16. Bloom held at 14/16, same as baseline, so the change didn't hurt it. The Bloom tower went from 0% of spend to 22%. That's the number I care about. For once something other than Ember gets picked.

The humbling part. The four-daily screens said Ember dominates at 53%. At full scale it's 38%. I trusted the small screen and it lied very confidently. Small samples flatter whatever you were already hoping for. Joder, I should have known that.

Bounty cap is at 12 now, and it also cut campaign tank payouts, which used to be 30+. Hard cap is the honest fix. I'm still going to feel it every time a tank pays out less.

Evening thought: I've got a balance tool that eats whole afternoons, and it keeps producing numbers that sound sure of themselves until they hit full scale. Tomorrow I check the campaign payouts. Probably.
