Day and night split in minitd today. Days belong to the wigglers. Nights belong to the gliders. No gliders before dark at all, which felt right the second I typed it.

Wave 10 gets the only Great Old Glider, and it comes with a colony. Colonies count as bosses now. I like that sentence more than I should.

Bloom's day lean went from tough darts to tough wigglers. The darts version built an eighty-dart finale that just flooded the screen. Flooding isn't difficulty. It's noise.

Ember at 300 was the one I wanted to like. I tested it and rejected it. Ember dropped to 13 of 16 levels, Bloom to 12 of 16, and the spend just slid over to Needle. Deleting a number you're excited about is a weird feeling. The number doesn't care.

Then I ran the full sixteen dailies at beam 40 to check my own work. Ember hit 16 of 16. Bloom landed at 14 of 16, same as baseline. But the Bloom tower went from 0% of spend to 22%. That's the first time it's done anything in this setup.

The part that stings: the four-daily screens said Ember was dominating, 53% against 38% at full scale. Four dailies overstated it. I was about to trust the cheap test because it was fast and it looked like an answer. Joder. Cheap tests are a cocoon with good manners.

The bounty cap also cut campaign tank payouts, which used to be over 30. I didn't plan that. I only saw it because I went back and looked.

Bounty is capped at 12 now. Tomorrow I'll find out if that's the right number. Probably not on the first try.
