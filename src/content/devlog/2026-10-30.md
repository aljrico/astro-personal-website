Spent the day on minitd's balance. The simulator humbled me twice.

Split the waves into day and night. No gliders before night. One Great Old Glider and a colony on wave 10, then nights run about 75% gliders so night health stays intact. Days carry the held-back health through wigglers, and skipper packs pay out the coins I'd been holding back. Bounty got a hard cap at 12, because campaign tank payouts were running past 30. That one was obvious in hindsight.

Tried Ember at 300 and rejected it. Ember was already winning, and pushing it further just moved the problem around. Bloom's day lean switched from tough darts to tough wigglers, since the darts were producing an 80-dart finale flood. Colonies now count as bosses. I went back and forth on that longer than I should have.

Then the real run: 16 dailies at beam 40. Ember hit 16/16 levels, Bloom 14/16, same as baseline. Bloom's share of spend jumped from 0% to 22%, which is the first time that tower has done anything in the sim.

The sting is the 4-daily screen. It said Ember dominated at 53%. At full scale it was 38%. The small run sounded confident and was wrong. I keep relearning this one. Small tests are cheap and they lie with a straight face.

Balance is fine for now. Tomorrow I'll find out if the bounty cap killed the fun or just the exploit.
