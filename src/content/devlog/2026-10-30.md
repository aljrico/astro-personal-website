Day and night, finally. Nights bring the gliders, days bring the wigglers. Before this, everything came at once and difficulty was just a number I kept nudging. Now it has a shape.

No gliders before night. The only Great Old Glider shows up on wave 10, with a colony beside it. Nights are about 75% gliders, which keeps the night health where I wanted it. The health I was holding back gets paid out by day through wigglers, and the coins I was holding back come from skipper packs. Bounty has a hard cap of 12 now, because without it the payouts ran away from me.

Bloom's day lean changed too. It used to be tough darts, and that made an 80-dart finale. That's not a challenge, that's a flood. Switched to tough wigglers. Colonies count as bosses now, which should have been obvious sooner.

Tried Ember at 300 and rejected it. Ember dropped to 13/16 and Bloom to 12/16, and the spend just slid over to Needle. Fine. Gone.

Then the real run: 16 dailies at beam 40. Ember hit 16/16, Bloom 14/16, same as baseline. Bloom's tower went from 0% to 22% of spend, and that's the number I care about. A tower nobody builds is a tower nobody is thinking about.

The uncomfortable part: the 4-daily screens lied. They said Ember dominated at 53%. At full scale it was 38%. I trusted the small sample because it was fast and it told me what I wanted to hear. Joder, I know better than that.

The bounty cap also trimmed campaign tank payouts, which used to hit 30+. That's what the cap costs, and I'm not sure yet I like the trade.
