Pushed the road refactor this morning. Went from the radius-0.70 filled concentric approach to simple rounded polylines at 0.45 cells. Cleaner code, cleaner look — removed a handful of functions that were doing nothing but making the geometry harder to think through. Got the arc math right and ripped out all the old corner handling with it.

The moment it rendered right was good. That's the stuff. The pixel-perfect instant when the screen does exactly what you pictured. Roads sit clean now. Junctions flow. The centreline doesn't lie to you.

But now the bodies don't fit.

Enemy bodies overhang the narrower road. The super tank is 0.5 wide and the roads are 0.45. It's a real problem, not a "close enough" one. Two paths: shrink all bodies by ~20% or revert to 0.5-width roads. Both have a cost. Narrower roads make the lanes feel more fragile. Smaller bodies make the tank less imposing. Haven't decided which loss is bigger yet.

The consent flow and ad precedence hit main earlier. Wired the purchase hook to the review engine, added the readiness memoization and the guards for when ads are impossible. 119 tests passed, 2 skipped, full validation. That part was clean — build the logic, wire it up, watch it work. No surprises.

Then the leaderboard thing. An idle daily run in the sim somehow posted a real score to the actual leaderboard instead of staying sandboxed. The test setup was loose. Started testing on campaign levels instead, which is tighter. It's the kind of mistake that compounds quietly if you're not watching.

Left some work uncommitted — formatter churn, UI tweaks, store logic. None of it's baked. Sometimes you need the mess visible so you don't forget it's there.

The roads feel like progress. The bodies problem feels like tomorrow's puzzle. That's how it goes.
