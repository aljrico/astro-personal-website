Balance day in minitd. Not glamorous. Spreadsheets and simulated runs, which is exactly the kind of work that eats an afternoon without anyone noticing.

Survival now alternates day and night. Nothing flies before night. Wave 10 gets the one Great Old Glider plus its colony, and night waves are about three quarters gliders, so night finally feels like night. Days are wigglers, which carry the health the gliders held back, and skippers pay out the coins that got held back. Bounties got a hard cap of 12, because without it the payouts ran away.

Bloom's day lean went from tough darts to tough wigglers. The darts version built an 80-dart finale that flooded the screen. Colonies now count as bosses, which I should have decided weeks ago.

I tried Ember at 300 and rejected it. Ember dropped to 13 of 16 levels, Bloom to 12 of 16, and the spend just moved to Needle. Not the answer.

Then the real run. Sixteen dailies. Ember hit 16 of 16, Bloom stayed at 14 of 16 like baseline, but Bloom's tower went from 0% to 22% of spend. That's the number I wanted.

The part that stings: the 4-daily screens lied. They said Ember dominated at 53%. At full scale it was 38%. Four samples made the problem look bigger than it is, and I was about to tune against the wrong picture. Joder. Small sample, big confidence. Classic.

The bounty cap that saved the economy also cut campaign tank payouts, which used to be 30-plus. So I traded one imbalance for a quieter one. Whether players feel it is a question for playtesting, not for me at midnight.
