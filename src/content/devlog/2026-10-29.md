Today was about the night. Survival has day and night now, and the real question was who shows up when.

Gliders wait for the dark. There's one Great Old Glider, and it shows up with a colony on wave 10. Nights are about three quarters gliders, so the night still has teeth. The day gets quieter, and the health and coins that used to come with the gliders have to go somewhere. Wigglers carry the health by day. Skipper packs carry the coins. Bounty got a hard cap at 12, because without it the payouts ran away from me.

Bloom's day lean moved from tough darts to tough wigglers. The darts made the finale flood, about eighty of them at once. That's not a fight. That's a screensaver. Colonies count as bosses now. The moment I wrote that rule down, the finale felt less like noise.

Ember at 300 I tested and threw out. Ember was already winning, and the spend just moved to Needle. Sixteen dailies at full beam: Ember 16 of 16 levels, Bloom 14 of 16, same as before. But Bloom's tower went from zero to 22 percent of all spend. I grinned at the screen like an idiot. That's the number I wanted.

The uncomfortable part: the four-daily screens said Ember dominated at 53 percent. At full scale it was 38. The small screens lied, and I believed them. Joder. The cap also trims the campaign tank payouts, which used to hit 30 and more. Good for balance. Bad for the part of me that likes big numbers.

Nights are in. Whether anyone will care about the nights is a different question, and today I didn't have an answer for that.
