Survival days got their day/night split today. Nights are the gliders. Days are the wigglers and the skipper packs, which pay out the health and coins I'd been holding back. Wave 10 gets the only Great Old Glider, plus a colony. Colonies count as bosses now. That's the kind of rule you add at four in the afternoon and then wonder why you didn't have from the start.

Bloom's day lean went from tough darts to tough wigglers. The darts were producing an eighty-dart finale flood. Not fun to watch, and worse, not fun to be on the receiving end of. I liked the wigglers before I had numbers for them. Liking something isn't evidence, but it's where I start.

Ember at 300 I tested and rejected. Ember sat at 13 and 16 levels, Bloom at 12, and the spend just moved over to Needle. So the thing I was worried about was fine, and the thing I was worried about was somewhere else.

Then the full run. Sixteen dailies at beam 40. Ember 16/16, Bloom 14/16, same as baseline. Bloom's tower went from zero to 22% of spend, which is the number I actually wanted. Good.

But the four-daily screens had Ember dominating at 53%. At full scale it was 38%. The small screen lied to me, and not by a little. Joder. I trusted the fast version because it was fast. I keep learning this every few weeks.

The bounty cap at 12 also cut campaign tank payouts, which used to hit 30 and up. The cap works. What I don't know yet is whether the 30s were the bug or the design. My guess is I keep the cap and feel a little sad about it.
