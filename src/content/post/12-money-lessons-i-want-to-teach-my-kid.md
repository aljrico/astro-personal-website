I wrote this list in 2023, before I had a kid of my own. Now I do. These are the lessons I'd hammer into their brain, so they never start losing money in the first place.

**1. Stop hoarding cash.**
Inflation erodes its value. You may think it's safe, but it's not. It won't disappear overnight, but every day it's worth a little less. Don't fool yourself into thinking it's safe.

**2. Save a percentage of everything you earn.**
The exact number doesn't matter. Pick one you can stick to. It's a personal decision, and nobody can tell you what percentage you should be saving. Be a grown-up and promise yourself a savings rate you can keep.

**3. Invest the money you save.**
The exact asset doesn't matter much either. Take the money you saved and do something with it. That's how you protect it from inflation. Pick whatever compounds capital and put the money there. Pick something you're comfortable with. You'll have to live with it.

**4. Don't reinvent the wheel. Buy ETFs.**
Unless you're willing to put in the effort, don't overthink simple stuff. Most index ETFs will outperform most wealth managers. Unless you're confident you can beat them too, don't complicate your life.

**5. Manage your own money.**
Don't pay someone else to underperform the index. If they manage to get any alpha at all, their fees will eat it. And that's the best-case scenario. More often than not, their alpha is zero or negative.

**6. Don't try to time the market.**
You will never win this game. Not even the top 1% have cracked it, and you most certainly won't. Accept it, and stop losing money trying to be "smart".

**7. Stop watching financial news.**
In finance, the important stuff doesn't happen overnight. It builds up over years. Stay informed, but never feel you need to act "immediately" on every new piece of information.

**8. The short term is unpredictable, but the long term is unavoidable.**
Understand this and you'll beat most people. Stop trying to make sense of daily movements. They're meaningless. Focus on yearly trends and learn to see the big picture.

**9. Don't act on emotions.**
Easier said than done. We're all emotional. Stop pretending you're different. We're all victims of hope, greed, fear, love and despair. Accept it, and learn to recognise when you're being irrational, so you can stop yourself before doing something stupid.

**10. Live below your means.**
The only realistic way to build wealth is to save consistently, and the only way to do that is to spend less than you earn. That's true whatever your income. Someone can earn ten times your salary and still be broke.

**11. Be a jack of all trades.**
No field of knowledge is completely disconnected from the others. No expert ever fully understands their own field without learning about others. Clear thinking comes from learning across many disciplines.

**12. Inaction can be the wisest action.**
Finance punishes overactivity. The more you do, the worse you'll tend to perform. Investing is a game where you move your pieces once a year, not once a week.
