## Advertising

Tiny TD uses AdMob to show interstitial ads between runs. The Google Mobile Ads SDK may automatically collect and share an IP address, app interactions, diagnostic information, and device or app identifiers for advertising, analytics, and fraud prevention. The app requests non-personalised ads and does not ask for tracking permission or use the advertising identifier to personalise ads. Where regional law requires consent, Google's consent form is shown, and you can change your choice at any time under Settings › Privacy › Advertising privacy choices.

A one-time purchase removes these ads permanently.

## Analytics

The app collects pseudonymous product analytics to understand how the game is played and to improve it. This includes gameplay events (levels started, towers built, runs finished), performance and thermal state, session timestamps, app and platform version, a coarse country derived from your device language, and a random identifier created for this app installation. No account or login is required, and this analytics does not collect your name, email, contacts, photos, precise location, or advertising ID.

Analytics events and the app-scoped install identifier are sent over an encrypted connection to my backend, provided by Supabase. They are used only for product analytics. They are not used for cross-app tracking, are not joined to advertising identifiers or to your leaderboard identity, and are not shared with advertising partners. Raw events are retained for up to one month and then reduced to aggregate daily counts.

You can turn analytics off at any time in Settings › Help improve the game. This stops collection and discards any queued events.

## Purchases

RevenueCat processes an anonymous app user identifier and purchase history to validate the Remove Ads purchase, restore it on your other devices, prevent fraud, and provide purchase analytics. While analytics is on, estimated ad impression revenue is also reported to RevenueCat against that identifier. Payments themselves are handled by the Apple App Store or Google Play; I never see your payment details.

## Leaderboards

Daily survival rankings and weekly leagues are optional. Until you open a leaderboard for the first time, none of the information in this section is collected.

When you take part, the following is stored on my backend (provided by Supabase) and tied to an anonymous account created automatically for your device:

- a random anonymous account identifier (this is not your Apple or Google account, and not an advertising identifier);
- the public nickname you choose;
- your daily survival times and weekly league points.

Your nickname and results are shown publicly on the leaderboard to other players, so keep the nickname free of personal information. This information is used solely to operate the leaderboards inside Tiny TD. It is not used for advertising, is not linked to any advertising identifier, is not used to track you across other apps, and is not sold or shared with third parties for their own purposes. Rankings are kept while the game is available.

You can delete this account and everything tied to it at any time from inside the app: Settings › Privacy › Delete leaderboard data. Deletion is immediate and permanent; a new anonymous account is created only if you join a leaderboard again. You can also request deletion by email using the address at the end of this policy.
