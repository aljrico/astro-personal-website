Accounting

Accounting is a private tool operated by Alejandro Jiménez Rico to organize his invoices, prepare accounting records and track revenue from his own projects. It is used with accounts he explicitly authorizes.

Privacy policy · Contact

Privacy policy

The tool uses Google permissions for these purposes:

  • Gmail: read billing messages and invoice attachments. The tool does not modify mail.
  • Google Drive: read, create and organize accounting files.
  • AdMob: read app identifiers, reporting settings and revenue reports for the portfolio dashboard.

Storage and access

Invoices and accounting records are stored in the operator's private local workspace and Google Drive. Aggregated AdMob reports are stored in a private Supabase backend and displayed through an authenticated dashboard. OAuth credentials are kept in private local or server configuration.

Access is limited to the operator and people he expressly authorizes for accounting or maintenance. The tool does not sell Google user data or use it for advertising or training generalized AI models. Google Drive and Supabase provide the storage described above.

Use of Google user data follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Retention and removal

Records are retained as needed for accounting, reconciliation and applicable recordkeeping obligations. Google authorization can be revoked in the Google Account settings. Revoking access stops future collection; it does not automatically delete previously collected accounting records.

For privacy questions or requests to remove stored data, contact aljrico@gmail.com.
